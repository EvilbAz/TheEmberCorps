## Make your first Hunter
{:#five-minute-version}

You begin with **500 XP at Stray rank**. Make a copy of either character sheet, then work down the list below. Both sheets are linked on [Getting Started]({{ '/start/' | relative_url }}).

### Choose what you want to do
{:#buildcraft}

Start with something you’d like to do in a scene. “I track the beast and trip it when it turns on our medic” gives you two things to look up straight away — the Survival skill for tracking and Foot Sweep for fighting up close.

1. **Choose your heritage.** You have 6 Unique Points (UP). In Standard Mode, pick a Primary Heritage and pay its listed UP cost. In Realistic Mode, spend 1 UP to roll your heritage. That mode grants bonus XP and charges Base XP for rerolls. [Choose your Uniques](#choose-your-uniques) before assigning Skill Points. You can save leftover UP. [Heritage costs]({{ '/rules/clans/#heritage-cost-index' | relative_url }}) · [Creation modes]({{ '/rules/core/#character-creation-modes' | relative_url }}) · [Uniques]({{ '/rules/uniques/' | relative_url }})
2. **Set your attributes.** Strength (STR), Resilience (RES), Chakra (CHA), Dexterity (DEX) and Agility (AGI) start at 20 each. Distribute another 25 points, adding at most 10 to any one attribute. For the tracker above, DEX helps the sweep connect and AGI helps them act early and Dodge.
3. **Assign your skills.** At the usual starting 500 Base XP, you have 25 Skill Points. One point buys one rank in any of the 14 skills, up to your rank limit. Bonus XP doesn’t grant more Skill Points. Start with the [Basic Training priorities](#basic-training-first) below. [Skills and limits]({{ '/rules/core/#skills' | relative_url }})
4. **Spend XP and choose gear.** Buy attributes, abilities and jutsu. Pick an attack you qualify for and check how you’ll defend yourself. The basic Dodge is already available. If you want a weapon, you can buy a finished one from the catalogue. [Learning jutsu]({{ '/rules/jutsu/' | relative_url }}) · [Equipment]({{ '/rules/equipment/' | relative_url }})

### Choose your Uniques
{:#choose-your-uniques}

A **Unique** is a trait you buy with Unique Points (UP). It can change the actions you can take, the skills you need or what happens when a roll succeeds. Two Hunters can buy the same Dodge abilities and still fight differently. Give one of them **Blur**, for example, and a fully successful Dodge lets them immediately move as far as a Speed 4 Move. That matters to an archer trying to open a gap. [Blur]({{ '/rules/uniques/#blur' | relative_url }})

**At creation, spend your 6 UP in this order.**

1. Pay for your Primary Heritage. In Standard Mode, pay its listed cost. In Realistic Mode, the heritage roll costs 1 UP total.
2. Decide whether you want any **Creation Uniques** before you finish the sheet. Each costs 1 UP. You may take up to two in total, including any **Clan Creation Uniques** you qualify for. For example, a Nara can spend one of those picks on **Long Shadow** to extend the range of shadow techniques and bend them around visible corners along a connected shadow. [Creation list]({{ '/rules/uniques/#creation-uniques' | relative_url }}) · [Clan Creation list]({{ '/rules/uniques/#clan-creation-uniques' | relative_url }})
3. Use your remaining UP on ordinary **1-, 2- or 3-point Uniques** you qualify for, or save them. For example, a 2-UP Heritage and two Creation picks leave 2 UP. You could buy one 2-point Unique, take two different 1-point options, or keep the points for later. You can normally take each Unique once.

**Choose around an action you expect to use often.** Read the drawback before spending the point, then apply any changes to your skills before allocating Skill Points.

| If your Hunter wants to… | Pick to consider | What changes in play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold the line | [Defender]({{ '/rules/uniques/#defender' | relative_url }}), Creation | Block becomes a Speed 4 Interrupt. Damage you take counts as 25% lower for Wounds when you Block. Your own attacks also count as 10% lower for Wounds. |
| Track beasts and cast elemental jutsu | [Naturalist]({{ '/rules/uniques/#naturalist' | relative_url }}), Creation | Survival 5 meets the ordinary D-rank elemental skill requirement by itself. You can build around tracking and still learn elemental techniques with the appropriate Aptitude. Your maximum Research falls by 3 and you take −3 on Genjutsu rolls. |
| Treat allies and make precise attacks | [Surgeon]({{ '/rules/uniques/#surgeon' | relative_url }}), Creation | Surgeon brings Medicine up to at least 5 without spending Skill Points. Your damaging attacks count as 20% higher for Wounds, but you take −1 on defensive rolls. |
| Switch weapons to suit the quarry | [Weapon Trained]({{ '/rules/uniques/#weapon-trained' | relative_url }}), 1 UP | Draw and sheathe at Speed 0. Buy Weapon Focus once and apply it to the weapon you draw. You can pick this up after creation. |

**Plan one upgrade ahead.** You gain 1 more UP at each of 2,000, 3,000, 4,500, 6,000 and 7,000 total XP. Spend UP between combats and events. To upgrade, meet the destination’s requirements, choose one eligible Unique you own and pay the difference in cost.

For example, a Hunter with **Surgeon** (1 UP) and **Medicine 8** can spend **1 more UP** to upgrade to **Combat Medic** (2 UP). They gain Combat Medic’s treatment benefits and keep Surgeon’s effects, including its −1 to defensive rolls. Buying Combat Medic directly costs 2 UP, still requires Medicine 8 and gives only Combat Medic’s effects. On your sheet, record the original Creation pick beside the current Unique. It still occupies one of your two Creation slots through later upgrades. Ordinary source effects are replaced unless the upgrade explicitly keeps them. [Upgrade paths and inheritance]({{ '/rules/uniques/#keeping-creation-effects' | relative_url }})

### Train Basic Training first
{:#basic-training-first}

**For most new Hunters, put 5 ranks each in Awareness, Espionage and Stealth, then spend 10 XP on Basic Training 1.** That uses 15 of your starting 25 Skill Points and leaves **10 points for your build**. Basic Training gives +1 Accuracy, +1 to defensive rolls and +1 to skill checks per purchased rank. It helps whether you swing a weapon, cast from the back line or spend the scene tracking a beast.

Your lowest of those three skills sets the limit. At Awareness 10, Espionage 10 and Stealth 5, you can still buy only Basic Training 1. Once all three reach 10, you can buy rank 2. A bonus to a *roll* helps resolve the check. When an ability asks for *ranks*, use the ranks on your sheet. [Basic Training]({{ '/rules/core/#basic-training' | relative_url }}) · [Skill Focus]({{ '/rules/core/#skill-focus-skill' | relative_url }})

**How Survival helps you Dodge.** At Survival 5 you can spend 6 XP on **Instinct 1** for +1 Dodge, or 10 XP on **Guarded 1** for +1 Parry. You can buy both. Survival 10 raises each purchase limit to 2. A hunter with Basic Training 1 and Instinct 1 has +2 on a Dodge roll from those abilities. Later, **Advanced Survival Tactics** offers another Dodge bonus once you have Basic Survival Tactics 5 and enough DEX for a rank. [Instinct]({{ '/rules/core/#instinct' | relative_url }}) · [Guarded]({{ '/rules/core/#guarded' | relative_url }}) · [Advanced Survival Tactics]({{ '/rules/core/#advanced-survival-tactics' | relative_url }})

### What each skill buys you
{:#skill-uses}

| Skill | When you use it | What to look for on your build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Search for a hidden enemy or watch a delayed attack for a tell. | One of the three Basic Training skills. Scouts also use it to beat an opponent’s Espionage with **Read the Windup**. |
| Espionage | Pick locks, sabotage a mechanism, set or disarm traps, or misdirect a watcher. | Counts toward Basic Training. Every 5 ranks allows another rank of **Cunning** for +1 Accuracy. At 20, **Cunning Blow** opens up a counterattack option. |
| Stealth | Hide, cross cover with **Quiet Advance**, or lay a false trail. | Counts toward Basic Training. Ambushers use Stealth to stay hidden long enough to set up a Sneak Attack. |
| Survival | Track a quarry, pick a route through rough ground, forage or bait a beast. | Every 5 ranks raises the limits for **Instinct** (Dodge), **Guarded** (Parry) and **Escape Artist** (Grapple Defense). Survival 5 also qualifies you for **Basic Survival Tactics**. |
| Athletics | Vault an obstacle, climb or help a Prone ally stand. | **Healthy** improves Stamina rolls, **Runner** improves Move actions and **Grip Fighting** lets grapplers spend AP to gain Control on eligible moves. Athletics limits all three. |
| Resistance | Resist physical statuses and poison, or **Brace Yourself** against Knockback. | Grapplers use it to buy **Pressure** for +1 Grapple Offense per rank. Blockers can buy **Fortitude** to improve Resistance rolls while blocking. |
| Chakra Control | Steady a chakra device, trace a fault or keep your handseals going if you take damage. | Pairs with Ninshou for elemental jutsu. Every 5 ranks permits a rank of **Refined Chakra Control** for +1 on Chakra Exhaustion rolls, up to rank 10. **Wall Walking** requires 8. |
| Ninshou | Read visible seals or a maintained technique, then explain its counter to an ally. | Elemental jutsu check both Ninshou and Chakra Control individually **and** their sum. Ordinary D-rank requirements are at least 5 in each and 15 combined. |
| Diplomacy | Negotiate, rally bystanders or challenge someone to hear an offer. | Diplomacy 4 or Research 4 qualifies you to buy **Genjutsu Training**. Diplomacy also sets the limit for **Genjutsu Competence**, which improves offensive Genjutsu rolls. |
| Research | Decode a seal, compare attack patterns or investigate a lead. | Every 5 ranks permits a rank of **Attentive** for +1 to Genjutsu rolls, and **General Knowledge** for better Medicine, Survival and Toxicology rolls. Seal specialists later need **Skill Focus: Research 5** for Advanced Seal Training. |
| Lore | Identify a monster from signs, recognise an old crest or recall an earlier hunt. | Lore can replace the listed skill on **any Hunt roll**, including Track, Research, Study Weakness and Prepare. A hunt specialist can cover all four with one investment. |
| Medicine | Diagnose a Wound. With 1 rank and a First Aid Kit, **Stabilise** can stop non-Wound Bleed or give treatment a Wound specifically permits. | Medicine limits **Doctor** for long-term care and **Anatomical Knowledge** for Taijutsu Wounding. Medicine 5 is also a prerequisite for **Senbon User**. |
| Artisan | Craft or repair gear in your chosen discipline: Weapon, Armor or Craftsman. | Ranks determine which patterns you can make. For example, Artisan (Armor) 3 qualifies you to craft Hunter Half-Plate. Pick the discipline that covers the gear you want to build. |
| Toxicology | Identify a toxin’s effects and route of exposure, or neutralize a coating before it is applied. | Check the rank on the compound or delivery tool you intend to use. For example, the Taper Inhaler requires Toxicology 5 and the Splitflow Injector requires 10. |

### Spend your next 10 Skill Points
{:#skill-builds}

These are starting spreads after setting aside 15 points for Basic Training. Each spends the remaining 10. Buy the abilities you want separately with XP.

| If you want to play… | Try these ranks | What they let you work toward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A weapon fighter or skirmisher | Survival 5, Athletics 5 | Buy Guarded if you usually Parry, or Instinct if you Dodge. Healthy helps you keep paying Stamina for attacks. |
| A grappler | Resistance 5, Athletics 5 | Buy Pressure for Grapple Offense and Grip Fighting for Control. If escaping holds matters more, swap Athletics for Survival 5 and buy Escape Artist. |
| An elemental caster | Chakra Control 5, Ninshou 5 | You meet both individual D-rank minima. You still need 5 more combined ranks under the ordinary requirements, plus the relevant Elemental Aptitude. Refined Chakra Control is already available. |
| A Genjutsu specialist | Diplomacy 5, Research 5 | Buy Genjutsu Training for entry-level techniques, then choose Genjutsu Competence for offense or Attentive for a bonus on all Genjutsu rolls. |
| A scout or hunt leader | Survival 5, Lore 5 | Use Awareness to Search in combat, Survival to follow a trail in the field and Lore across the Hunt rolls. Survival also opens Instinct for when the quarry turns on you. |
| A field medic or poison specialist | Medicine 5, Toxicology 5 | Carry a First Aid Kit for Bleed and field-treatable Wounds. Use Toxicology to identify what poisoned a patient and Medicine to assess their condition. |
| A crafter who fights with their own gear | Artisan 5 in your chosen discipline, Survival 5 | Read the pattern’s Final AP before buying ranks. Pair your crafting investment with Guarded or Instinct for the fights where you use that gear. |

### What the numbers on your sheet do
{:#sheet}

| When you need to know… | Look at… |
| --- | --- |
| How hard you are to hit | **Dodge** and your other defenses. Basic Dodge uses AGI. |
| How hard your attack is to avoid | **Accuracy.** This is usually the number the defender must match on their roll. |
| How much damage you deal | Your attack’s **damage dice** and the relevant **Damage Bonus**. PDB is Physical Damage Bonus. NDB is Ninjutsu Damage Bonus. |
| How much damage you can take | **Vitality** first, then **Hit Points (HP)**. Record Wounds separately. |
| Whether a technique tires you out | Your **Stamina** or **Chakra Exhaustion** roll, including your current penalties. |

### Rank and class
{:#ranks}

**Stray** is your Hunter’s standing in the Corps. **E-Rank** on a jutsu is that technique’s tier. A **Hunter Class** is a specialty you can develop as you advance. [Hunter ranks and classes]({{ '/rules/talents/' | relative_url }})

### Get your sheet reviewed
{:#first-day}

Send your sheet to a GM for review. A sentence about who your Hunter knows in Haven Ember or why they signed up gives the GM something to use in play. While you’re under 1,500 XP, you can rework your sheet between combats and events.

## Join a scene on Discord
{:#ic-and-ooc}

**IC** in a channel name means **in character**. Write what your Hunter says or does there. **OOC** means **out of character**. Use the matching OOC channel to ask the GM a question or talk to the other players.

> **In the hunt channel:** “Those tracks stop at the water.” Ren crouches on the bank to look for prints on the far side.
>
> **In hunt OOC:** “Can I roll Survival to see where it came out?”

## Make a skill check
{:#skills}

Describe what you’re trying and how. If the outcome is uncertain, the GM can call for a skill and set a **Target Number (TN)**. Roll **1d20 + your ranks in that skill**, with any modifiers that apply. Meeting the TN succeeds. With no ranks, you can still roll the die.

At the riverbank, the GM calls for **Survival, TN 15**. Ren has 4 ranks and rolls 11. That’s 15, enough to pick up the trail. What the tracks reveal is up to the GM.

## When do I act in a fight?
{:#speed}

In combat, **IC** means **Initiative Count**. Everyone shares one clock. Roll initiative with **1d20 + AGI/10**. The highest roll starts on IC 0. For everyone else, the difference from the highest roll is their starting IC. If Ren rolls 16 and Aya rolls 13, Ren starts on 0 and Aya starts on 3.

**Speed tells you when you can act again.** Add your action’s final Speed to the count you acted on. A Speed 4 action on IC 0 puts your next action on IC 4. The action usually takes effect right away. **Delay** tells you if the effect lands later. A Speed 12, Delay 4 attack declared on IC 10 lands on 14, and your next action is on 22. Characters due on the same count act simultaneously.

### Post your opening block
{:#combat-block}

Agree on a landmark and put everyone on the same map. “Five yards away” gets confusing as soon as a third Hunter moves. “Ren at the gate, Aya 5 yards west of the gate” lets everyone work out range. Roll initiative, then give whoever is tracking the fight one line they can paste:

`[Name] | Next IC __ | Position __ yards east/west of __ | Vitality __/__ | HP __/__ | WP left __ | AP __ | Fatigue __ | Stamina penalty __ | Chakra Exhaustion penalty __ | Upkeep __ | Conditions and expiry __`

After an action, post just what changed. “Aya next IC 5, still at the gate, spent 1 AP” is enough for the tracker. If a technique lands later, give it a separate entry on the clock: “Attack lands IC 14. Ren acts again IC 22.”

### Five yards apart and your turn is up
{:#first-ranged-turn}

Ren wins initiative **21 to 19**. He starts at the gate on **IC 0**. Aya starts five yards west on **IC 2**. Ren takes a Speed 5 Rest, so he's due again on **5**. Aya gets to act on **2**. She can use a Speed 7 attack here. The three counts before Ren's turn tell her how soon he can answer, not how much Speed she's allowed to spend.

Aya knows Water Sphere. Before posting, she checks three things:

1. **Can it reach?** Water Sphere reaches 15 yards, so five is fine. If she wanted to punch instead, she'd measure how far she can close. With 25 AGI, a Speed 8 basic unarmed attack carries her up to **2 yards** toward Ren. From five yards out, she'd need to move closer first. [Move with an Attack]({{ '/rules/core/#move' | relative_url }})
2. **Are her hands free, and when is she ready?** At zero gallons, Water Sphere has Seal Speed 5. With 30 DEX, Aya subtracts 3, leaving **2 counts of handseals**. She posts `(IC 2 · Perform Handseals · Speed 2 · ready IC 4)`. With a modified Seal Speed of zero she could form the signs and attack on IC 2. If she takes damage while sealing, check Chakra Control before carrying on. [Perform Handseals]({{ '/rules/core/#perform-handseals' | relative_url }})
3. **What does the defender need?** On IC 4, with Ren still in range, Aya posts: *Aya cups the water between her palms and sends it at Ren.* `(Water Sphere at Ren · 0 gallons · range 15 yd · Chakra 5 · Speed 7 · ACC 13 · next IC 11)`. Her usual Accuracy is 10 + 3 from DEX. Ren rolls against **13**. Aya makes her separate Chakra Exhaustion check against **5**. [Water Sphere]({{ '/rules/jutsu/suiton/#mizudama-no-jutsu-water-sphere-technique' | relative_url }})

Ren can defend on IC 4 even though his turn is due on 5. He posts `(Dodge · Speed 2 · 0 AP)` and rolls 11 + 2 for AGI = **13**. That matches Accuracy. Once both players settle any Willpower use, the dodge stands and there's no damage to roll. Ren changes his scheduled IC from **5 to 7**. Aya stays on **11**. If she passed her cost check, she records **1 Chakra Exhaustion penalty** for her next chakra check, even though Ren dodged. Both stay where they were. **Ren acts next.** [Willpower]({{ '/rules/core/#willpower-2' | relative_url }})

### Read the jutsu before you declare it
{:#reading-jutsu}

Take [Deashi Harai – Foot Sweep]({{ '/rules/jutsu/unarmed/#deashi-harai-foot-sweep' | relative_url }}). Its E-Rank badge tells you what tier you’re buying. Its **Attack** and **Melee** tags tell you it’s an attack you use up close. The entry gives **Speed 4**, **Stamina 8**, **Accuracy +2** and **Special: Prone**. Outside a grapple, a full hit knocks the target Prone. If you’re already grappling, use the separate effect in the entry.

### Play out one exchange
{:#defence}

Ren and Aya start a spar in melee range. Ren has 30 DEX. Aya has 20 AGI. Neither has other modifiers in this example.

| Count | What the players do |
| --- | --- |
| **0 · Ren attacks** | Ren posts **(Foot Sweep against Aya, Speed 4, Stamina 8, Accuracy 15)**. His usual Accuracy is 10 + 3 from DEX, and the sweep adds 2. He makes his Stamina roll against 8. His next action is on **4**. |
| **0 · Aya defends** | Aya can respond now, even though her first action was due on 3. She declares **(Dodge, Speed 2)**. A Defensive Interrupt normally has to be half the incoming action’s Speed or less, so Speed 2 fits. |
| **0 · Resolve the sweep** | Aya rolls 13 on the die and adds 2 from AGI. Her 15 matches Ren’s Accuracy, so she stays on her feet. Add the Dodge’s Speed to her **scheduled** action. She was due on 3 and now acts on **5**. |
| **4 · Ren acts again** | Ren is up first. If Aya takes another Interrupt, update her count again. |

**Dodge, Parry or Block?** Dodge can avoid a hit. If an eligible Dodge misses by one, you take 20% of the effects under Partial Success. A Parry must be able to meet the incoming attack, especially against an area attack. A basic Block reduces damage and requires Prepare Blocking to be in effect. A Guard Shield gives you its own Weapon Block. [Defending and Partial Success]({{ '/rules/core/#defending' | relative_url }})

**Action Points (AP)** let you change the timing. You normally start combat with 0 and gain 5 at IC 20, 40 and each multiple of 20 after that, up to AGI/2. Spending 1 AP can cut an action’s Speed by 1, subject to its minimum. [Speed limits]({{ '/rules/core/#flow-of-time' | relative_url }}) · [Action Points]({{ '/rules/core/#action-points-2' | relative_url }})

**When is 1 AP worth spending?** Against Ren's Speed 4 Foot Sweep, the ordinary Speed 3 Parry is too slow. If Aya has 1 AP and a suitable Parry, she can declare `(Parry · Speed 3 reduced to 2 · spend 1 AP)`. If her Dodge bonus is just as good, the Speed 2 Dodge already fits for free. On attack, reducing Speed 6 to 5 can force an opponent to spend AP on that same Parry, but it usually costs you one Speed's worth of Damage Bonus. Check the defender's options before spending.

### Damage, Wounds and Fatigue
{:#damage}

Damage comes out of Vitality first. If you have 12 Vitality left and take 20 damage after defenses, lose the 12 Vitality and mark 8 damage against HP. A single hit can also cause a **Wound** while you still have Vitality. Its severity depends on how much of your maximum pool the hit took. [Wound thresholds]({{ '/rules/wounds/#wound-categories' | relative_url }})

**Stamina 8** on Foot Sweep means you roll against TN 8. For a technique with a Chakra cost, use your Chakra Exhaustion roll against that cost. A failed check adds one Fatigue level, plus one more per full 15 points you missed by. The action normally resolves. At Fatigue 5, another level incapacitates you. If the same check pushes you past incapacitation, the technique also fails. Record the cost divided by 5, rounded down, as a penalty to later rolls of the same kind. If Ren passes the Stamina 8 check, he marks 1 Stamina penalty. Next time he rolls Stamina, he subtracts 1. A basic Speed 5 Rest clears one point of that penalty. When Fatigue rises, both accumulated penalties reset. Update the sheet before your next declaration. [Fatigue and Upkeep]({{ '/rules/core/#fatigue-2' | relative_url }})

## Between scenes
{:#xp}

XP you earn can buy another technique, an ability or better attributes. Spending XP doesn’t erase the XP you earned toward progression. Spend points between combats and events. [Experience and advancement]({{ '/rules/core/#spending-experience-points' | relative_url }})

### Roleplay
{:#roleplay}

You can start an ordinary scene with another Hunter. Bring in a GM when you need opposition, a ruling or information about the world. Active in-character interaction earns **4 XP per 30 minutes**. A scene that changes a relationship or reveals something important can also qualify for a Roleplay Beat. [Roleplay rewards]({{ '/rules/core/#roleplay' | relative_url }})

### Missions
{:#missions}

A GM gives the team a job, such as escorting a caravan or finding a missing patrol. Check the posting for the objective and who can join. Mission rewards normally include XP and Ryō.

### Hunts
{:#hunts}

On a hunt, the team goes after a monster. You might research what injured the last party or track where the creature feeds before you face it. You can spend Downtime on preparation. Joining the hunt itself costs no slot. [Hunting rules]({{ '/rules/hunting/#starting-the-encounter' | relative_url }})

### Downtime
{:#downtime}

You get **two free slots each real-world week** for work such as training, research or crafting. Use those two before the week ends. Extra slots earned from play can be saved. Completed spars can keep earning slots, with more spars required for each successive slot that week. Log what you did and link any required rolls. Completing an action that uses a slot earns **5 XP**. [Downtime rules]({{ '/rules/core/#downtime-2' | relative_url }})

### Learning a technique
{:#jutsu}

**Taijutsu** covers physical techniques like Foot Sweep. **Bukijutsu** uses weapons. **Ninjutsu** shapes chakra into effects such as a clone or a blast of fire. **Genjutsu** interferes with what a target perceives. Find the school you want in the compendium. Check its learning requirements, then read the technique’s costs, tags and effects. For ninjutsu with a Seal Speed, allow time for handseals before using the technique. [Jutsu Compendium]({{ '/rules/jutsu/' | relative_url }})
