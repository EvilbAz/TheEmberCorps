## Haven Ember
{:#haven}

Several thousand people live at Haven Ember, an independent settlement built over a pre-Fall transport hub. Its underground water system still supplies the Haven. Old sealing arrays reinforce the inner walls. The outer districts grew up around them, built largely from salvage.

### Inside the walls

Hunters bring their kills to the monster-processing hall. Smiths and mechanics use the recovered materials in their workshops. The Ashen Anvil supplies weapons and field gear. Caravans bring in medicine, food and machine parts that Ember cannot readily replace when a road closes.

When the Crown Beneath broke through the walls, it destroyed the Market and the Graveyard. The Academy, Great Gate, civilian shelters, Archives and Hospital survived. Hunts resumed while the Haven was still rebuilding. The [timeline]({{ '/timeline/' | relative_url }}) records the breach and the expeditions that followed.

### The Ember Corps

The Corps trains new Hunters, known as Strays. Veterans teach in the training yards and lead expeditions. Crews form for a particular job. A caravan escort needs scouts who know the road. A missing patrol may need a tracker and a medic. When a migration threatens the walls, the same Hunters can be called home to defend them.

## The Wastes
{:#world}

Outside settled territory, the old roads run through nesting grounds, abandoned villages and industrial ruins. Merchants share them with scavengers, raiders and other expeditions. A route that carried wagons last season can be lost to new growth or cut off by a migrating herd.

### Ruins and salvage

Old workshops still hold tools and medicines that few Havens can make. Sealed archives may hold the only surviving copy of a clan technique. Some sites remain guarded by the traps and defence systems their builders left behind. Others have become dens.

### Roads between Havens

Havens govern themselves. Some maintain roads for trade, while others keep their gates closed. News travels with convoys or by carrier bird. Ember learned that Haven Enryu had fallen to a Calamity when birds arrived from the west carrying a report and a sample of white quartz.

## The Bloom and the fall of the Five Great Nations
{:#history}

Before the Bloom, the Five Great Nations maintained Hidden Villages at Konoha, Suna, Kiri, Kumo and Iwa. Their Kage commanded shinobi armies. Clans passed down techniques that survive in Ember families today, long after the borders those families once defended disappeared.

Accounts disagree on when the Bloom began. Natural chakra surged through soil, water and living things. Forests overtook settlements and rivers changed course. Animals developed armour, regeneration and elemental abilities. Some became intelligent. As villages lost contact and trade failed, survivors gathered around wells, farmland and whatever defences they could repair. Those settlements became the Havens.

### The Ember Lord and Genzo Arakane

The Ember Lord subdued Ikidomari when she nearly became a Frenzied Hunter. Afterward, her battlefield service was restricted and she took up medical logistics. Genzo Arakane belongs to her generation, a connection known to few outside the Corps’ senior ranks.

## Maps and records
{:#open-records}

Hunters bring route reports back to Ember. Beyond the roads the Corps maintains, there are abandoned strongholds and stretches of country for which the Archives have no reliable map. A recovered manuscript may still use the name of a village that vanished during the Bloom.

The Bingo Book records people. Hunters consult it to recognise a dangerous opponent or put a name to someone mentioned in another Haven’s reports.
