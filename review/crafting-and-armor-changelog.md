# Crafting and pre-made armor

## Wielding weapons and wearing armor

You can use a weapon of any Final AP. The Athletics + 10 cap and its over-limit penalties are gone for melee weapons, ranged weapons and thrown weapons. This also covers the Bonded Insect Glaive and Ballistic Corps firearms. Your Artisan ranks still limit the weapon you can make. Weapon-specific handling and Heritage requirements still apply. You can also wear armor of any Final AP. The Resistance + 10 wear limit and its penalties are gone. Artisan ranks still limit the armor you can craft.

## Making your own gear

- Materials for weapons, armor, ammunition and ordinary gear cost one eighth of the shop price. Use the listed price for a pre-made pattern or the frame and AP formula for a custom build. Apply your crafting discount, then round up to the next whole Ryō. Poisons and antidotes use their own recipes.
- Weapons and ammunition drop from TN 15 + 4 × AP to **10 + 2 × AP**. Armor drops from TN 15 + 3 × AP to **10 + 2 × AP**. Use Final AP for equipment and Ammo AP for a batch of ten arrows or bolts.
- For ordinary gear, halve the old materials bill, from one quarter of retail to one eighth. Divide the total shop price by 400 for the crafting TN (previously 200), rounding up. Four Flash Bombs now cost **750 Ryō at TN 15**. Custom Smoke Bombs start from the correct **1,500 Ryō** shop price before upgrades.
- Spend a downtime slot, roll Artisan and pool rolls across slots until you meet the TN. Pay for materials when you finish. Artisan ranks, special ingredients and Master Artisan requirements still apply.
- Reforging now spells out the credit for your old piece. Subtract 75% of its materials cost and 25% of its TN from the new pattern. The finished item keeps the original structure, features and properties and replaces the old item.
- Hunter Half-Plate drops from **4,000 Ryō at TN 24** to **500 Ryō at TN 16**. The Black Rain Launcher drops from **22,750 Ryō at TN 67** to **1,657 Ryō at TN 36**.
- Updated the printed crafting TNs for Splitjaw Switch Axe (51 → 28), Basalt Breaker (47 → 26), Carapace Guardlance (63 → 34), Jawbone Cleaver (39 → 22), Raptor Fangs (47 → 26) and Ridgeback Heavy Bowgun (47 → 26). The illustrated weapon and armor catalogues now use the rulebook’s shop prices.

Each weight now has two choices for each of the four slots. Arm and leg pieces cover both limbs. Bleed, Burns and Stun reductions apply to hits on the covered location. Knockback reduction applies from any source.

A crafting audit confirmed all 24 patterns’ AP budgets, defensive stats, prices and crafting TNs. The cards now give each leg piece its own Knockback percentage, spell out the arm armor blocking tradeoff and distinguish built-in bonuses from the totals shown.

All 24 patterns have illustrated item cards showing weight, slot, shop price, materials, crafting TN, AP, Durability, Absorption, Immobilization and Stealth. Each card opens that piece’s full stat block. You can also find individual patterns by name in rulebook search.
