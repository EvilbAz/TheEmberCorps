# Sage traditions — player changelog

- **Wild** can change aspect when declaring Nature Pulse for +2 Speed. The new aspect applies to that Pulse and stays selected even if the Search fails.
- **Great Toad** can hold Steady for 10 IC while stationary in Mode. You can Gather or attack from your spot and keep the protection. Reduce an incoming shove before checking whether it moved you. Picking Springing replaces Steady.
- **Serpent** can spend 1 Sage Chakra when declaring Break Clinch or Escape Pin to add Sage Power to the escape roll. On success, slip up to 2 + Sage Power yards. The normal roll, Stamina and Delay still apply.
- **Roc** keeps the existing perch-to-dive handoff. A stationary Dodge pushes back the count when you dive. If your target moves, measure the whole approach from your perch before committing.
- **Tortoise** can carry Stoneback’s DR into its next Prepare Blocking or Still Shell Gather. The DR protects against the hit that ends the preparation. Moving, leaving Mode or finishing the preparation ends the extension.
- **Moth** with Cocooned Stillness can Improve Hiding from Stealth 2 to 3 while in Mode. Pay Speed 7 and apply the usual action penalty. Guards who already found you keep sight of you.
- **Carrion** can learn Red Trail on its own. Spend 1 Sage Chakra at Speed 4 to Search for injured quarry within 20 yards per Sage Rank. Nature Pulse still lets you Search for healthy creatures and other chakra sources.
- **Fungal** Spore Veil hangs for 10 IC, even if the network breaks or Mode ends. You can use Hyphal Snare on your next action if you can perceive a legal target. Each Sage can maintain one veil.
- **Salamander** can give up Regrowth’s ally heal to shed 1 severity from the affliction being transferred. Any remainder keeps its original tick schedule. Rest sheds another 1 + Sage Power severity when declared in Mode. Regrowth still starts the ally’s 20 IC cooldown.

Worked turns for all nine traditions appear under **Sages on the clock** on the Natural Chakra page.
