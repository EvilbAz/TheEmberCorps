# Ember Corps wording pass
10 November 2026

## Starting out

- The homepage now points new players straight to making a Hunter. The New Player Guide gets to Discord scenes, XP and Initiative Counts earlier, and cuts repeated advice about learning the whole book.
- The starting guide, lore introduction, Minecraft server page and Cinder Runs each have a shorter introduction. The historical timeline and NPC records retain their original wording.

## Rules at the table

- Core combat now gives the Dodge roll directly, introduces Multi-Throw in one sentence and explains Knockback in yards from the attacker or the jutsu’s point of origin. The guidance on movement and defensive jutsu is shorter.
- Team Attacks now tells each Hunter when to finish handseals, commit ammunition and check Range. The Hunt introduction distinguishes preparation from departure. Skill Actions puts the opposed roll, fatigue cost and Speed spent on failure together.
- Wound severity points to the highest threshold reached. The equipment catalogues say what you can buy and where to find the full entry.
- Uniques opens with what a Unique gives you and where your Heritage Cost comes from. Clan entries cut repeated explanations of Heritage restrictions and simplify the Vanguard and Great Sword descriptions.

## Jutsu and Talents

- The elemental chapters explain practical questions directly, including what counts as a water source, how to declare Gallons, when fans count as free hands and how Raiton behaves in water.
- Medical Ninjutsu puts self-treatment and the recipient’s Upkeep together. Several weapon and Taijutsu entries now describe the actual motion, such as drawing a guard with one blade or pinning a hand against the chest.
- Feral Release introductions describe what a Hunter sees or feels when a Scar forms. Each school still links to the shared Scar rules.
- All 21 Tamed Monster profiles have individual introductions. The crocodile waits for a bite, the elk carries a Hunter over rough ground, and the tortoise shelters an ally. Their profiles, commands and stat lines are unchanged.

## Checks

Compared rule numbers, table rows, navigation targets, and inline scripts and styles against the previous revision. The search index contains the same 3,347 destinations. The rules validator checked 78 source pages. All nine site tests passed, including search, rank navigation, browser history and music playback across page changes.
