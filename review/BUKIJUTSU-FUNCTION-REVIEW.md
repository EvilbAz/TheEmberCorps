# Bukijutsu function and Chain review

Reviewed all 145 learnable entries. This pass changes 26 techniques, including all 17 Thrown entries. Damage growth from the Taijutsu comparison remains; the new Chain reward grants no passive damage multiplier.

## Chain decisions and counterplay

| Situation | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Chain 1 hits, then Chain 2 hits | Value reaches 2; gain one Opening. |
| Repeat Chain 2 three times | Value remains 2; no additional Openings, dice growth or universal Speed reduction. |
| Multi-hit Chain 2 lands twice | One Chain advance and one Opening, not two. |
| Use the Opening on an ally’s Ninjutsu | Ally receives Assist; no Bukijutsu requirement. Your Chain retains its value but its Opening is spent. |
| Spend Control on your non-Chain Attack | Apply the declared Special bonus; the Chain then ends normally. |
| Declare a Chain 3 and miss | Chain and unused Opening end; spent Opening is not refunded. |
| Use Crossfire Cast to change targets | Old Chain and Opening end; new target begins at Chain 1. |
| Enemy blocks, dodges, retreats or disarms | Normal defenses and range remain; full miss of Chain 2, loss of weapon and other printed break conditions still end the Chain. |

## Stacking checks

- A +6 Bukijutsu stance, +4 preparation and +6 technique grant **+6**, not +16, to the same roll. Ordinary weapon statistics and non-Bukijutsu abilities retain their own rules.
- Two −6 Bukijutsu Defense penalties apply **−6**, not −12.
- Extra projectile allowances of +3, +4 and +6 from Bukijutsu grant **+6**, not +13. Ammunition and normal Multi-Throw costs remain.
- A repeated Defense is paid and advances time once when Manipulated Shuriken says to reuse it. It cannot be chained with another reroll effect.
- Returning Arc’s separate second strike has a new Defense and cannot also demand a reroll.
- A Bukijutsu counter can be defended; that Defense cannot generate a counter-counterattack.

## Representative sequence

At STR 90 with a 4d8 / Speed 10 / STM 12 sword, without Focus, stance, AP or mitigation: Rising Cut averages 90 damage at Speed 9 / STM 15; Descending Cut averages 100 at Speed 11 / STM 17 before its optional damage rider; Final Measure averages 278.75 at Speed 14 / STM 36. The three-attack sequence costs Speed 34 / STM 68. Three Basic Attacks cost Speed 30 / STM 36 and average 204 total damage. This is a cost comparison, not equal-resource or accuracy-adjusted playtesting. The Chain buys stronger offense and one Opening with higher expenditure and target commitment; basic attacks retain their cheaper cost.

## Purpose of the review

Multi-target coverage, selectable damage types, defensive access, item delivery/recovery, armor interaction, timing pressure, statuses, information and Chain management are meaningful functions. A technique does not need an extra status rider when one of those functions already gives it a reason to be chosen. The following inventory records the role of every entry.
