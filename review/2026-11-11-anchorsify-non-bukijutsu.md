# Non-Bukijutsu rules pass · 11 November 2026

## Genjutsu at the table

| Initial roll against Offense 22 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Defense 22 or more | The defender wins. |
| Defense 17–21 | The illusion takes hold. The victim recognizes it at once and can try Kai. |
| Defense 16 or less | The victim believes it. An ally can still help, and the technique may give the victim another chance to notice. |

- Removed the separate IC recognition countdowns. Use the initial margin. Haze Clone still rewards Observe, Mirage can be exposed by physical contact, Sly Mind allows hourly checks, and sleeping victims still need outside help to use Kai.
- Familiarity rises from +3 to +5 per successful casting of the same jutsu in combat. Use it on later defenses, including release rolls, even against another caster. Repeat checks on one casting never build more familiarity.
- Any real Wound now frees the victim from ordinary illusions, including Area effects on that victim. A successful Area Kai still dispels the whole casting. Illusory Wounds deal no HP damage and cannot trigger release.
- Combat illusions normally expire when the fight ends. Maintained non-clone Area illusions, sleep, Fog of War, Stun techniques and helpless-subject techniques use their own clocks. Durations still begin on application.
- Kai uses the original Offense, the roller’s familiarity and the bonus bought with Chakra. Removed the extra +2 per consecutive Kai. The victim rolls for a personal release. The Kai user rolls to dispel an Area. An ally can help an unaware victim by touch.
- Direct, Indirect and Area resolution now spell out who rolls and when. Ties favor the defender. A technique’s printed Accuracy modifies the casting roll.

## Individual illusions

| Jutsu | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Illusory Mist | Follows the caster. Failed initial defenders take −4 Visibility and −2 Genjutsu Defense inside it. Leaving suspends the penalties. Returning restores them while affected. |
| Sly Mind | Travel loops last 30 minutes per point of margin, replacing the fixed day. The hourly recognition roll and Wound exception still apply. |
| Fog of War | Requires 2 available Willpower and charges them only on success. Incoming danger still frees the victim before defense. |
| Brain Destruction | Margins 1–4, 5–9 and 10+ inflict Minor, Major and Severe illusory Wounds respectively. The caster chooses any Wound of that severity, including type and location. At Severe, Upkeep is 8 and Duration is 40 IC. The wound cannot directly knock someone unconscious. |
| Phantom Pain and Iron Maiden | Removed the old Major-only release threshold. Both follow the shared real-Wound rule. |
| Five Monkey Curse | A victim ignores Minor Wound penalties, including existing ones. While the curse lasts, a new Minor Wound cannot free the victim from any illusion. Major or worse still frees them. |
| Tree Binding Death | Prevents voluntary movement, but forced movement still works. Every 10 IC the victim may check to recognize it, at +1 on the first check, +2 on the next, and so on. A successful check opens Kai. |
| Double False Surroundings (new B-rank Link) | Give one place two false appearances. Pay for both layers at casting. Clearing the first exposes the second to a fresh defense. Both share an Area and clock. A real Wound clears both for one observer. |

Hallucinatory Voices, Mist Servant, Descending Hell, Hell Viewing, False Battlefield, Echo Chamber, Drowning Dream, Killing Intent, Nervous Convulsions and Bringer of Darkness also lose their individual recognition timers. Drowning Dream keeps its real-Suiton bonus. Nervous Convulsions still gives a release roll after an affected action.

## Specialist and heritage interactions

- Shadow Clones start at Fatigue 1 and disperse on damage or a failed Fatigue roll. They copy existing Wounds and self-affecting Preparation Upkeeps only. Each pays for its copied preparations. Basic Dodge and basic Parry cost no command AP, but delay its next action. Other commands retain Ember’s 1 AP per 5 Speed. Clone Upkeep tracks surviving clones.
- Legion Method now discounts the combined Upkeep while at least three clones from its casting survive. Its old discount referred to an upfront Chakra cost the jutsu did not have.
- Eight Gates self-Wounds trigger the ordinary Genjutsu release. Sage Sense may locate a caster without revealing the illusion. Across Abyss, Cinder, Glass, Storm, Vacuum and Void, applying a Scar alone cannot trigger a release. A Scar effect that inflicts a real Wound can.
- Added resolution types to Shiin Borrowed Voice, Memory Projection, Broken Refrain and Memory Blocking, Yamanaka Mind Body Switch and Mind Disturbance, and the Sharingan’s false-opening Genjutsu. Mind Body Switch uses Genjutsu Offense with its printed Accuracy. Its specific possession escape still costs 1 Willpower after a Major or worse Wound.
- No existing jutsu was removed or reranked. Previously learned techniques and old page anchors still work. Rasengan, Ōdama Rasengan, Chidori and Renkuudan retain their published charge profiles.

## Reference and balance decisions

The recognition margin, familiarity, Wound release and end-of-combat exceptions follow [Anchorsify’s combat rules](https://anchorsify.github.io/Combat/). The technique comparisons use its [Jutsu catalogue](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/), [Genjutsu](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Genjutsu/), [Eight Gates](https://anchorsify.github.io/EightGates/) and [Kage Bunshin](https://anchorsify.github.io/KageBunshin/).

Double False Surroundings is limited to two appearances of one place in Ember. It cannot chain area-control attacks. Flawless Diagnosis uses +10, between Ember’s former +5 and Anchorsify’s +15. Shocking Burst’s average printed dice fall from 16.25 to 8.75 before damage bonuses, in exchange for the faster pulse and stronger condition. These are rules and arithmetic checks. Encounter balance still needs table play.
