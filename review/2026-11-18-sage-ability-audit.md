# Sage ability audit · 18 November 2026

Checked the nine signatures, 40 tribal arts and 23 general arts and kata against the combat clock, action costs, targeting, status rolls and legal learning paths. These are worked rules cases, not live encounters. Sage Power 1 is used below unless another value is stated.

## Roc timing

| Position and declaration | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| IC 13, 3 Sage Chakra, 25 AGI. Step to 10 yards over a target. | Spend 1. Stay at 10 yards through IC 16. Declare Talon Dive on a basic unarmed attack, spend 1, descend 10 yards and land. Speed 9 puts the next main action on 25. Landing occurs on a miss too. |
| Same opening. At IC 14 Dodge in place at Speed 2. | The next action moves from 16 to 18. The perch lasts to 18. Dive then and act again on 27. A Wing Guard Dodge that actually moves off the perch gives it up. |
| Power 3, Step straight up 20 yards. | Talon Dive has 20 yards of approach from the perch. It reaches a target directly below. A target 20 yards down and 10 yards sideways requires more path than that allowance. |
| Gather from the perch with Hang Upon the Sky. | Support changes from Step to Gather on declaration. At rank 4, Perfect Communion makes Gather Speed 9, Delay 7 before further modifiers. Gather declared on 16 resolves on 23. The renewed perch covers the wait to 25. An abort ends Gather’s support. |
| Spend the last point on Step, Wait while perched, or get knocked off. | Mode loss, Wait or displacement releases the perch. Without another source of support, start normal Falling at the current height. A rescue Interrupt must reach a willing falling ally and a surface for both within one Step. Carry on the Wind likewise needs a landing for both. |

## Tribal checks

| Tradition | Cases and outcomes |
| --- | --- |
| Wild | Open Communion grants one aspect. Nature Pulse uses Survival and its normal cost, Speed, range and surface requirement for terrain reading. A concealed trail uses its existing TN. An empty search area can return no sign. |
| Great Toad | Steady protects until the next main action. Springing combines Pond-Leap with one attack. Ripple Strike plus Springing at Power 1 moves the target 4 yards with one shove and at most one Prone roll. Still-Water Listening permits a same-surface Search without sight. Broad-Backed Guard needs a successful interception. Heaven-Shaking Landing requires a legal landing and melee target and retains the basic attack’s Stamina cost. |
| Serpent | Coiled Reach can pull the user into an empty melee space on a clear path. Shed the Binding may clear Immobilization and move, but a separate Pin still holds. Venomous Communion needs damage through mitigation. Constricting Coil needs a full hit and failed Resistance. Patient Predator preserves the TN against the Gather action only. Perfect Shedding cannot clear a Wound. |
| Roc | Sky-Borne Balance limits ordinary Move. Updraft Step, Talon Dive, Wing Guard, Carry on the Wind and Hang Upon the Sky use the cases above. Sky-Borne Balance gives no standing licence to hover. |
| Tortoise | Ancient Carapace improves a prepared Block. Still Shell preserves the first Block per Gather, subject to concentration. Intercepting for the ward costs the Gather even on a miss. Stoneback supplies flat DR before Block. Root the World needs an existing Resistance roll. Sheltering Carapace supplies one Block after interception. Unbroken Shell protects one Gather from repeated Blocks and their damage checks. |
| Moth | Powder-Sense aids Awareness and defence. Dust Veil reports a crossing. Powder Mark can be used alone or shared. Moonwing Drift can Hide inside the veil or preserve existing Stealth level with its printed TN cost. Blinding Scales requires damage and failed Resistance. Cocooned Stillness preserves level 3 on Gather, without protecting other actions. |
| Carrion | Blood on the Wind recognises hunt injuries. Red Trail requires Nature Pulse. Scavenger’s Claim can designate one ally for a wound closing on IC 30 to exploit through IC 35 inclusive. Circle the Wounded can mark healthy quarry but cannot pursue until it qualifies. Scavenger’s Communion grants its point once per battle. Last Descent accepts a Critical hunt monster. |
| Fungal | Rooted Communion checks the surface under the Sage. Mycelial Ground resolves Resistance on placement. Link two patches no more than 15 yards apart across continuous ground. Spore Veil or Hyphal Snare can originate at the far patch. Snare still needs a perceived target and breaks beyond 2 yards from its anchor. Deep Roots and Perfect Communion each reduce Gather once. The Network Listens needs Nature Pulse and measures its doubled range from the chosen origin. |
| Salamander | Regenerative Balance works at zero Stamina penalty. Shed Pain permits standing with movement. Hardy Blood modifies an existing roll. Regrowth may transfer a status at full Vitality and records its ally cooldown even then. Molt Away checks the severity and removal restriction. Refuse the Ash needs 2 Sage Chakra and a qualifying Fatigue failure. |

## General arts and kata

| Checks | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Deep Reservoir, Swift Communion, Stable Balance | Apply passives during setup. At rank 1 Deep Reservoir raises the cap from 4 to 6. Swift Communion changes unmodified Gather from 10/8 to 8/6. Stable Balance reduces Mode Upkeep to 1. |
| Nature’s Eyes, Nature’s Understanding, Nature Pulse, Hunter’s Sense | Check the sense, task and detected target each effect names. Pulse uses one Awareness roll against existing Stealth TNs. A relayed bearing does not reveal the quarry to allies. Hunter’s Sense applies Sage Sense once. |
| Sage’s Correction, Sage’s Breath, Senjutsu Infusion | Correction follows a qualifying roll. Breath precedes the cost roll. Choose Infusion on attack declaration even when Accuracy is static. |
| Natural Kata, Nature Guard, Predator’s Step, Sage’s Recovery | Extended reach adds no threatened area. Guard modifies a legal Dodge or Parry. Step modifies movement distance. Recovery increases the Stamina penalty cleared by a completed Rest. |
| Stillness In Motion, Flowing Communion | Accumulate fractional movement across Gather’s Delay. With room in the pool, Flowing spends 1 AP on resolution for 3 total in Mode. Check final capacity after the gain. |
| Sage Reservoir, Nature Without Thought | Apply the first positive-cost discount, then check whether the remaining affordable cost would empty the pool. For example, at 2 points a 2-point art discounted to 1 leaves 1 and does not trigger Reservoir. A passive cannot consume the first-use discount. |
| Sage Strike, Sage Reversal, Earthen Root | Pay the base attack’s Stamina and use Strike’s Speed. Reversal moves or swaps after the successful melee Parry. Root can substitute one permitted Resistance roll for the adjacent ally’s roll. |
| Natural Shock, Sage Break | Shock at Power 1 deals 3d8 + NDB × final Speed. Trading the dice on a full hit leaves the NDB portion and an opposed push. Break at Power 2 bypasses 2 flat DR and reduces one defensive percentage by 10 percentage points. A successful Dodge still avoids the attack. |

## Changes made during this audit

- Removed the three-IC timeout from Roc perches. Clarified loss of support, last-point use, rescue order, Wing Guard movement, passenger landings and the handoff to airborne Gather. A dive from a perch can cover the full Step distance.
- Specified concentration and last-point resolution. Separately identified action Speed, linked Speed and effects riding another roll.
- Defined overlapping Toad Knockback, same-surface listening, post-hit Serpent escape timing, Tortoise Block percentages and failed intercepts. Made Powder Mark’s ally optional and identified who must fail against Blinding Scales.
- Clarified the Wild trail TN, Carrion injury gate for pursuit, inclusive handoff expiry, initial resistance to Fungal terrain, and Regrowth’s Self or Touch card and recipient cooldown.
- Matched Pulse to the core Search procedure. Removed double-counting from Hunter’s Sense, clarified fractional Gather movement and high-rank discount order, and gave Sage Break concrete flat and percentage mitigation steps.
