# Restart comparison and jutsu changelog · 11 November 2026

Reviewed the eight Ember Corps catalogues alongside the matching Shotai and Anchorsify chapters. All 268 Ember techniques keep their ranks and learning slots. Nineteen entries changed. The examples below use Ember’s rules and numbers.

| Catalogue | Reviewed | Revised | Retained examples |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Katon | 30 | 2 | Flame Whorl already spends Ignite; Living Bomb already lets its caster choose a timer. |
| Raiton | 36 | 3 | Racing Thunder already buys immediate movement. Chidori and Raikiri offer different release times. |
| Suiton | 35 | 2 | Water Shark Blast lets the caster ride the attack; Pool of Water already lets a team plan around ground hazards. |
| Doton | 37 | 3 | Earth Prison Dome commits the caster to maintaining contact. Earth Dragon Blast leaves terrain on a grounded hit. |
| Fūton | 33 | 2 | Gale Palm already assists a thrown attack. Renkuudan keeps its uncapped charge damage and range. |
| General Ninjutsu | 39 | 2 | Ink Clone already trades an action for a distraction. The Rasengan and Ōdama charge spikes carry over. |
| Fūinjutsu | 31 | 3 | Alarm, Tracking and Warding seals each have their own trigger or inspection rule. |
| Genjutsu | 27 | 2 | Descending Hell already gives victims a place and time to escape. Drowning Dream already lets victims spend an action to steady themselves. |

## Katon

- **Smokescreen.** The Link now has a declared center and radius. Choose one target’s position or the center of the linked AoE. Smoke occupies Area 3 × rank and stays at that point. The existing duration and Visibility scaling apply. The standalone Ignite version has Range 10 and an effective rank from 1 to 5, based on half Ignite rounded down. Shotai’s Enmaku specifies where a fire technique leaves its smoke; this version leaves one cloud even when the attack hits several enemies.
- **Mist Blaze Dance.** A damaging Katon or Ignite 5+ can set the cloud off. The explosion cannot set off another Mist Blaze Dance. Rank or one-third Ignite determines X, capped at 4; simultaneous triggers use the higher value. The blast now deals Energy damage and allows normal Speed 8 AoE defenses, including Dodge. The lingering Suffocation, expiry and overlap limits remain. Shotai distinguishes an immediate blast from a cloud opponents have already seen. Ember uses a single defense procedure in both cases.

## Raiton

- **Nerve Overloading.** Choose 1–4 severity from one timed Raiton Paralysis effect. Gain that much Accuracy. On a full hit, spend the chosen severity for Stun 3 per point, up to 12. A failed conversion leaves the condition intact. Previously the entire condition converted at 5 Stun per point with no listed cap. Shotai distinguishes a condition that improves aim from one discharged for Stun; Ember uses a single existing condition and lets the player decide how much to spend.
- **Wave of Inspiration.** Accuracy is −8 normally, −6 against a target struck by Suiton in the last 15 IC, or −4 through a shared conductor. Use the best one. You may spend up to 4 timed Raiton Paralysis for another +1 Accuracy per severity on a full hit. Previously the wet and conductor modifiers had no stated precedence and each severity added +2 before the whole status was removed. Base damage and double NDB remain. Anchorsify’s water follow-up informed the choice of position or prior setup.
- **Paralysis Break.** Select one target within its listed 20 yards. A hostile target gets a Projectile defense; a willing one can accept. Double one timed Raiton Paralysis effect up to severity 6, halve its remaining time rounded up, and alter that application only once. Severity already above 6 cannot be increased or lowered by the doubling. Previously it picked the strongest timed effect anywhere on the field and could repeatedly double it.

[Raiton in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/raiton-ninjutsu) · [Raiton in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Raiton/)

## Suiton

- **Water Splash.** Invest 1–3 gallons, down from 1–90. The first time an attacker sees the trick, gain +1 to +3 Dodge according to gallons invested. Later uses against that observer give +1. Removed the repeat Espionage roll and accumulating divisor. Chakra, added Speed, and Seal Speed still depend on gallons. Both references use a small Dodge bonus; Ember keeps water investment and lets an opponent learn the trick.
- **Hidden Mist.** Starts at Visibility −2. Every 10 IC of Upkeep it thickens by 1, up to severity 2 + floor(G/3). Both partial dispersal and regrowth use the existing mist. Complete dispersal ends the cast. Voluntarily dropping Upkeep leaves a stationary cloud that fades by 1 every 5 IC. This replaces immediate −G Visibility and free recasts. Anchorsify’s growing, recoverable mist inspired the timing. Ember uses gallons to set the ceiling.

[Suiton in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/suiton-ninjutsu) · [Suiton in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Suiton/)

## Doton

- **Earth Shaping.** Personal handholds retain +5 Athletics. Leave them in place and the next ally to use that stretch within 30 IC gets +3. Both references use shaped stone to help the caster climb; the smaller follow-up bonus gives an Ember scout a reason to go first.
- **Earth Shaking Palm.** Stun 5 affects other grounded creatures in the Area. The caster can bury up to two loose background gallons at impact and attempt a normal Hide. Carried water and standing bodies of water are protected. Shotai’s small water-removal effect becomes a limited interaction with Ember’s gallon economy.
- **Sticky Body.** Test once when a melee action actually makes contact. A missed or fully parried attack cannot stick to the caster. The existing opposed roll, +2 Grapple Offense, one-sided Clinch and 2 IC escape remain. This uses the contact distinction that the restarts also use for Mud Clone.

[Doton in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/doton-ninjutsu) · [Doton in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Doton/)

## Fūton

- **Four Seasonal Winds Palm.** Replaced reduced Blocking on all unarmed techniques with an optional Knockback 2 on a full non-grapple unarmed hit. The caster may follow the displaced target up to 2 yards along a legal path. One push and follow per attack, even with multiple hits or targets. Both restarts let a fighter follow the force of a palm strike. Ember keeps the movement small and respects terrain.
- **Tearing Gust.** A full parry stops the ranged Taijutsu attack. A partial parry can strip one non-Katon Ninjutsu Link before remaining damage is worked out. A miss strips nothing. The former version stripped every Ninjutsu effect even on failure. Shotai’s partial-parry interaction gives the roll three distinct outcomes.

[Fūton in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/fuuton-ninjutsu) · [Fūton in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Fuuton/)

## General Ninjutsu

- **Cicada Shell.** Activating while hidden preserves Stealth. The caster may make their voice appear to come from a fixed point within 10 yards. Hearing distance still measures from the caster, moving beyond 10 yards ends the projection, and other senses can locate the speaker. Both restarts protect a hidden speaker; choosing the apparent source is Ember’s addition.
- **Needle Hell.** Commit 1 or more points of Needle Jizo Upkeep and declare the line when attacking. Delay now equals points spent, replacing a flat 5. The line stays fixed, and defenders still inside can use normal AoE defenses. Existing barriers intercept under their own rules. Spending the last point of Needle Jizo still releases the volley. Damage, its NDB cap, and doubled target DR carry over. Anchorsify’s greater commitment for a larger volley inspired the timing.

## Fūinjutsu

- **Twin Rising Dragons.** Draw and attack from the two loaded scrolls in one action. Extra weapons count toward damage, Chakra and other costs. Calculate Speed using the original throw. Extras are limited by Chakra Control/5, available ammunition, and a total volley ceiling of the normal multi-throw limit +2. Pay the Link cost on each throw. Each projectile leaves inventory. Shotai’s prepared-scroll attack inspired the draw and ammunition rules.
- **Sleeping Moon.** Set a single observable trigger within 10 yards for a properly placed Mark or fixed Barrier. Pay placement costs and resolve any placement defense up front. You can exempt nearby signatures up to Research/5. The first trigger starts the effect on its original bearer or site and starts its Upkeep and duration. A dormant setup expires after 24 hours and can be unsealed. Removed elemental attack storage, remote application of Marks, and overlap with Warding Seal or Speed Cast. The references’ trigger conditions informed this split between switching on a seal and storing an attack.
- **Attached Meteor.** A full hit anchors a limb, dealing no damage. Immobilization starts at 2 and gains 1 per full 3 points of margin, up to 5. Movement otherwise unaffected by Immobilization is still reduced by 20%. Only a stronger hit replaces the tether. Removed the four discounted stacking casts and the Immobilization penalty on the victim’s escape roll. The victim breaks free beyond the anchor distance or with the Speed 6 opposed escape action. Shotai’s margin-based restraint inspired the single-tether version.

[Fūinjutsu in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/sealing-ninjutsu) · [Fūinjutsu in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/SealingJutsu/)

## Genjutsu

- **Hallucinatory Voices.** Select victims present when the jutsu resolves; each rolls Genjutsu Defense immediately against one casting roll. Formerly the text selected people already inside but deferred defense until entry. Leaving suspends the Search penalties, re-entry resumes them for an affected creature, and someone who escaped stays free of that casting. Both references use this as interference with repeated Searches.
- **Haze Clone.** Roll Offense once. Observe at +2 Speed lets each observer test Defense against that casting, beginning at −5 with +2 per earlier attempt by that observer this combat. Success grants Realization. Successful Kai lets that observer distinguish the caster while other victims still see the doubles. Replacing doubles raises Upkeep once per destructive action, down from once per clone destroyed. The Observe-to-Kai sequence and replacement cost draw on both restarts.

[Genjutsu in Shotai](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Jutsu/genjutsu) · [Genjutsu in Anchorsify](https://anchorsify.github.io/projects/Genjutsu/)

## Table checks

- **An ally’s setup.** A target struck by Suiton 8 IC ago with timed Raiton Paralysis 3 gives Wave of Inspiration Accuracy −6 before spending severity. Spending 2 gives −4 on the roll. A full hit leaves Paralysis 1 with its original timer; a miss leaves 3. A shared conductor instead starts the attack at −4.
- **Water and Wind.** At 9 gallons Hidden Mist starts at −2, reaches −3 at 10 IC, −4 at 20, and caps at −5 at 30. Reducing it from −5 to −3 buys two growth ticks. Complete dispersal requires a new cast.
- **A charged volley.** At NDB 5, X = 4 and unmodified Speed 15, Needle Hell averages 140 damage before DR: 100 from the dice and 40 from NDB. X = 10 averages 325. The larger line takes 10 IC to fire and costs 10 points of Needle Jizo; the smaller takes 4.
- **A bound target.** Attached Meteor gives Immobilization 2 at a margin of 1–2, 3 at 3–5, 4 at 6–8, and 5 at 9+. Landing a weaker second shot buys no additional restraint.
