# Bukijutsu / Taijutsu comparison

## Scope

Benchmarks use full-hit expected dice, STR/18 PDB truncated to one decimal, printed Speed and Stamina. No paid abilities, stances, Links, AP, defenses, armor or crafting bonuses. Weapon attacks use a 4d8, Speed 10, Stamina 12 medium weapon of the appropriate family. This is not a complete-build simulation or a live playtest.

Taijutsu benchmarks: Daisenpuu (C, STR/6 d12, Speed 14, STM 22); Gouriki Senpuu (B, STR/5 d12, Speed 16, STM 33); Solar Agitation (A, STR/5 d20 plus 5d20 at CC5, Speed 18, STM 40 including Combo cost). Solar also inflicts its Combo riders. Bukijutsu finishers require Chain 2; prerequisite attack costs are not included for either sequence.

## Interpretation

The new dice counts grow with STR, closing the widening high-STR gap. Medium weapons trade lower damage per action for lower Speed and weapon-specific effects. Large weapon dice increase the payoff further. Taijutsu retains Momentum, Cadence, special Links and powerful conditional effects; these examples do not establish equality across optimized builds.

## Effect changes

Short-step setups became Links that punish defensive timing, bind weapons, increase Grapple Control, or add resisted statuses. Crossbar Catch redirects projectiles. Through-Line penetrates armor and can strike a second target. Leaping Pole is now a damaging attack with a substantive vault/knockdown role. Ordinary Move with an Attack remains available; token post-action steps were removed.

## Sheet integration

The Jutsu Damage column resolves the new STR-based additional dice for populated rows 7–364. It still uses dS for the equipped weapon die size, as the sheet has no weapon selection per Jutsu row. Skills and Teamwork rows 50–68 provide a weapon/stat comparison calculator. Conditional effects remain resolved at their triggers.
