# Bukijutsu rank and scaling audit

Historical scaling-pass snapshot. See [BUKIJUTSU-FUNCTION-REVIEW.md](BUKIJUTSU-FUNCTION-REVIEW.md) for the latest complete role review.

All 145 learnable entries across eight weapon families and universal Bukijutsu reviewed. 118 entries revised. Costs remain as printed unless noted in the change record. This is a balance draft, not playtest evidence.

## Scaling conventions

- Athletics-based roll modifiers and extra weapon allowances: floor(Athletics/5), minimum 2, maximum 6.
- Movement and Reach scale from AGI, STR, CHA or weapon dimensions.
- Damage dice, PDB × Speed, percentage damage and Wounding retain their existing scaling.
- Weapon damage uses the defending weapon’s damage-level threshold.
- Small Speed modifiers remain where they materially change action timing; unchanged numeric costs are costs, not effect ceilings.

## Current technique effects

Current source after the Taijutsu comparison pass. Damage below is an addition to weapon damage unless the entry states otherwise.

### High Guard (D)

Stamina: 8

Speed: 4

Effects: Your Chain 1 and Chain 2 Melee Bukijutsu gain +Athletics/5 Accuracy. Your Chain 3 Melee Bukijutsu gain +15% Final Damage.

### Low Guard (D)

Stamina: 8

Speed: 4

Effects: Gain +Athletics/5 to targeted Melee Parries. After fully defending against an enemy Attack, your next Melee Chain Attack against that enemy gains +15% Final Damage if it is your next non-Interrupt Action.

### Long Guard (D)

Stamina: 9

Speed: 4

Effects: Increase your Melee Weapon Reach by 50%, minimum +2 yards. Gain +Athletics/5 Parry against targeted Melee Attacks that began outside your unmodified weapon Reach.

### Open Guard (C)

Stamina: 12

Speed: 5

Effects: After the target declares its Defense against your Chain Attack, impose a penalty of Athletics/5 on that Defense. The penalty applies once to each Attack, including one that generates multiple hits.

### Point Guard (C)

Stamina: 12

Speed: 5

Effects: Reduce Called Shot Accuracy penalties with weapon attacks by Athletics/5, minimum 0. Called Shots gain +15% Wounding.

### Committed Guard (B)

Stamina: 18

Speed: 6

Effects: Damaging Melee Bukijutsu gain +25% Wounding. Increase their printed Knockback by 50%, rounded down, and their printed Stun by half your PDB, rounded down. Apply these increases before Special rolls and Partial Defense.

### Weapon Recovery (E)

Stamina: 4

Speed: 3

Effects: Draw, pick up, or retrieve one weapon within arm’s reach without changing Chain value.

### Flowing Guard (D)

Stamina: 6

Speed: 3

Effects: Switch from one active Bukijutsu Stance to another you know without changing Chain value. Pay this technique’s Stamina and Speed instead of the new Stance’s activation costs. The old Stance ends and the new Upkeep begins.

### Weapon Breaker (B)

Stamina: \+10

Speed: \+2

Effects: Use retroactively after your weapon Attack is successfully Parried or Blocked by a weapon. After that Defense resolves, inflict WDP equal to half the defending weapon’s damage-level threshold, rounded up. Use that weapon’s Durability + its wielder’s DEX/10. This is explicit weapon damage and applies once to that weapon per Attack.

### Chakra Reinforcement (B)

Speed: 10

Effects: Choose Blunt, Slashing, Piercing or Energy. One held weapon may deal that wound type and gains a Damage Bonus equal to half your NDB. Calculate this bonus when activating the technique, before bonuses granted by maintained weapon techniques.

### Chakra Reach (A)

Speed: 10

Effects: One Melee Weapon gains additional Reach equal to the greater of its unmodified Reach or your CHA/5. When striking through this extension, it does not trigger on-contact punishment caused solely by touching the defender. Attacks still need a clear path.

### Rising Cut (E)

Damage: +max(2, floor(STR/15))dS

Stamina: \+3

Speed: \-1

Effects: On a full hit, note the Defense used. If the target uses that broad Defense against your next Sword Chain Attack, it takes a penalty of Athletics/5 and its final Speed is doubled when advancing its next action. This does not affect Interrupt validity.

### Draw Cut (E)

Damage: +max(1, floor(STR/15))dS

Stamina: \+4

Speed: \+0

Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. Reduce this Attack’s final Speed by 2 after modifiers, subject to normal minimums. If the target has not yet attacked you this combat, gain +25% Wounding. The sword ends drawn.

### Descending Cut (E)

Damage: +max(3, floor(STR/15))dS

Stamina: \+5

Speed: \+1

Effects: If your previous Chain attack was Chain 1, choose one when this Attack fully hits: Drop Weight — treat this Attack as 4 Speed higher for damage only; or Smother the Guard — note the Defense used against this Attack; that same broad Defense takes −Athletics/5 against your next Sword Chain attack.

Damage: +max(5, floor(STR/15))dS

Stamina: \+8

Speed: \+3

### Wire Cage (A)

Stamina: 20

Speed: 10

Effects: Requires four thrown weapons and enough Ninja Wire to connect the Area’s perimeter. Area is the greater of 6 yards or DEX/5. The cage lasts until its wire or anchors are cut, destroyed or retrieved. Creatures inside have 50% less movement. Crossing its boundary requires Athletics against your Athletics; on failure the creature stops at the boundary and becomes Prone. A creature may attack a wire or anchor using normal object rules to open a passage. Resolve one crossing check per movement Action.
