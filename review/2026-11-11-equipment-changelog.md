# Equipment changelog · 11 November 2026

Ten new pre-made weapon patterns are available to buy or craft. The Buckler also joins the existing Tonfa pattern. Prices for matched weapons cover the pair.

## Monster-hunting weapons

| Pattern | Catalogue class | Price | Speed / damage | Build | AP / crafting TN |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Splitjaw Switch Axe | Large | 16,500 Ryō | 13 / 7d10 | Honed Geometry 1, Broad Edge, Transforming Frame | 9 / 51 |
| Basalt Breaker | Large | 15,500 Ryō | 13 / 7d10 | Impact Chamber, Armor Breaker 1 | 8 / 47 |
| Carapace Guardlance | Large | 19,000 Ryō | 13 / 8d10 | Layered Construction, Guard Mount, Driving Point | 12 / 63 |
| Jawbone Cleaver | Extreme | 23,500 Ryō | 15 / 9d12 | Heavy Head 1, Forward Balance | 6 / 39 |
| Raptor Fangs | Matched | 11,500 Ryō | 8 / 3d6 | Lightweight 1, Serrated Teeth, Dual Balancing | 8 / 47 |
| Ridgeback Heavy Bowgun | Crossbows | 11,750 Ryō | 16 / 12dS | Reinforced Winch, Stabilised Sight | 8 / 47 |

- **Splitjaw Switch Axe.** Spend a Speed 2 Utility action to change forms. The greatsword uses Sword Bukijutsu at Reach 2, Accuracy +0 and Parry +0. The extended axe uses Polearm Bukijutsu at Reach 3, Accuracy +1 and Parry −2. Both get +10% Wounding on Slashing damage. Crafting requires Master Artisan.
- **Basalt Breaker.** Deal 25% more damage to Armor and Weapon Durability. This stacks with Club under the usual durability rules. If an Attack already has Knockback, spend +3 Stamina when you declare it to add 2 yards. Apply the extra distance after a successful status roll and before Partial Success.
- **Carapace Guardlance.** Its shell guard gives you a Speed 5 Weapon Block for 25% damage reduction. Gain +1 Accuracy when the target starts the attack within your Reach but outside its own natural Melee Reach.
- **Jawbone Cleaver.** For damage, count 1 extra effective Speed, capped at half the attack’s Final Speed. On a Basic Weapon Attack you can also take +1 Final Speed for one extra Damage Die.
- **Raptor Fangs.** Use the usual Dual rules. If a Slashing Wound from the pair applies Bleed, add 1 Bleed, once per Attack. Crafting requires Master Artisan.
- **Ridgeback Heavy Bowgun.** This Heavy Arbalest takes ordinary Bolts. A standard Bolt deals 12d10. Capacity 1, Reload 11 while stationary. On an Aimed Attack, ignore up to 2 points of Visibility penalty.

## Shinobi equipment

| Pattern | Catalogue class | Price | Speed / damage | Build | AP |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Knuckle Blades (Trench Knives) | Matched | 9,000 Ryō | 8 / 2d6 | Seal Grip, Dual Balancing | 4 |
| Folding Iron Fan (Tessen) | Small | 3,500 Ryō | 8 / 2d6 | Quickdraw Fittings | 3 |
| Guard Shield | Medium | 7,500 Ryō | 10 / 5d8 | Layered Construction, Guard Mount | 8 |
| Rain Umbrella (Ame-gasa) | Medium | 6,500 Ryō | 10 / 4d8 | Layered Construction | 5 |
| Tonfa / Buckler | Small | 4,500 Ryō | 8 / 2d6 | Hand Guard 1 | 3 |

- **Seal Grip** is a new 3 AP component for Small, One-Handed weapons. You can form handseals with that hand. Add 3 to Seal Speed once per action, even if both hands use Seal Grip. The hand counts as occupied for other actions.
- **Knuckle Blades** come with Seal Grip. Keep both drawn while forming handseals. Both hands still count as occupied for Unarmed, Combo and Grapple requirements. Buy or craft the pair as one weapon.
- **Folding Iron Fan** draws or sheathes at Speed 3. It uses Fan Bukijutsu, can Parry Weapon and Unarmed Attacks, and works as a focus for compatible Fūton techniques.
- **Guard Shield** gives a Speed 5 Weapon Block that reduces the triggering attack’s damage by 25%. You can use it without taking Prepare Blocking first.
- **Rain Umbrella** swings as a closed Club. Open it as part of a Speed 6 Weapon Block to reduce the triggering attack’s damage by 25%.
- **Buckler** uses the Tonfa’s attacks, Parries and Club Bukijutsu. The shared catalogue card now lists the rulebook price of 4,500 Ryō and shows both weapons.

## Catalogue and search

- The catalogue lists each pattern under its usual class. Open **Monster-hunting weapons (6)** at the top to jump straight to any of the six illustrated cards. Select a card for its full equipment entry.
- All six hunting patterns have individual illustrations. The Splitjaw shows both forms, the Basalt Breaker its flat face and chamber, the Guardlance its shell guard, the Cleaver its single jawbone edge, the Fangs their paired hooks, and the Bowgun its crossbow limbs, winch and sight.
- The Knuckle Blades card now shows the finger grips on both knives. The fan, shield and umbrella retain their existing illustrations.
- The equipment entries are indexed in site search.
