# General Ninjutsu, Fūinjutsu, and Genjutsu · 11 November 2026

If you learned **Killing Intent Projection (Sakki Hōsha)**, recover the XP you spent on it. The B-rank Genjutsu **Killing Intent (Sakki)** remains a separate purchase with its usual requirements. The old bookmark and search term lead to the refund note.

## General Ninjutsu

- **Killing Intent Projection (C)** was retired. It inflicted fear-based Immobilization through Resistance. The remaining Killing Intent uses the Genjutsu Offense, Defense, Realization, and Kai rules.
- **Temporary Paralysis (D)** now physically restrains with Immobilization 4 for 15 IC on a full hit. Resolve the usual status check. The victim can attempt Athletics against the caster’s Chakra Control as a Speed 5 action to escape. Reapplication refreshes the duration. Removed Stun 5 and the automatic Paralysis 4 that could last as long as another source of Stun.
- **Chakra Strengthening (C)** now makes the caster choose +1 to +5 Physical Damage Bonus or +1 to +3 Dodge. Previously it gave up to +5 to both. The 30 IC duration, cost of 5X, and Upkeep of 3 + X remain. Repaying the cost on expiry lets the caster choose the next mode and strength. The choice of Chakra or Stamina still lasts the fight.
- **Scattering One Thousand Crows (C)** can be Dodged. A full hit starts at −2 Visibility, then worsens by 1 every 10 IC to −4. Previously it rose from −1 to −10 over a 100 IC duration. The flock now lasts up to 30 IC. A damaging AoE covering the victim scatters it when the attack resolves, even if the victim avoids damage. Leaving Range or ending Upkeep also disperses it.
- **Clone Great Explosion (A)** commits one to three eligible clones of the same type. Measure travel from each clone and use the longest travel time for the Delay. Clones can be destroyed on the approach. Pay for committed clones, count only arrivals for the explosion, and apply NDB once per target. All committed clones disperse when the Delay ends. The blast allows ordinary defenses, including Dodge, followed by the existing Resistance check if hit. The caster can be caught. The elemental damage types and per-clone activation costs carry over.
- **Cloth Binding (A)** now uses the revised Five Elements and Influence effects below. Removed the special blanket bans on Chakra and Taijutsu. Sacred Silence still masks sound and chakra, and Evil Sealing Method still gives a 20 IC window to restore Upkeep. All four seals waive Upkeep only while all four remain. On losing one, resume Upkeep or release the captive, subject to that grace period. Removed the suspended-animation option that named unlisted techniques. The cloth breaks at 1,000 damage before reinforcement. The B-rank Strengthening Seal adds its actual HP and DR instead of making the cloth indestructible. The captive can still struggle, and an ally can undo the cloth in melee.

## Fūinjutsu

- **Closure Seal (D)** is the new name for the D-rank Strengthening Seal. It locks a door, lid, or similar closure for up to a week. The object can still be broken. **Strengthening Seal (B)** is the reinforcement that adds HP and DR. Existing characters keep their learned rank and the old links still work.
- **Tracking Seal (C)** now gives a concrete inspection action. A shinobi who knows C-rank or higher Fūinjutsu can find the mark in melee at Speed 10. An appropriate Unseal removes it. Ordinary sight and chakra senses still miss it.
- **Contract Seal (B)** targets one active summoning contract on a full hit. It dismisses creatures summoned through that contract and blocks further calls through it for 30 IC or until unsealed. Previously it dismissed everything already summoned and gave no end time for the disruption.
- **Five Elements Seal (A)** ends one active Upkeep of the caster’s choice. For 40 IC it imposes −6 Chakra Exhaustion and +2 to each Chakra Upkeep. Normal Exhaustion rules apply. Reapplication refreshes duration without stripping another Upkeep. Removed the permanent −25 Exhaustion, loss of 15 Chakra Control ranks, blanket Upkeep cancellation, and additional casting failure checks.
- **Wheel Devouring Vortex (A)** suspends one currently active clan jutsu or activated bloodline feature for 30 IC. Its duration keeps running, and the bearer must maintain its normal Upkeep if they want it back. A new Vortex replaces the previous one. Previously it disabled every clan ability and jutsu for one minute. Innate anatomy and passive features now continue functioning.
- **Influence Seal (A)** keeps its placement and activation costs. Arm it within 30 yards for 30 IC. The next Chakra-costing jutsu triggers one Chakra Exhaustion check against TN 2X. Failure cancels the jutsu before payment and advances the target by Stun 5 only. Success permits the ordinary jutsu and Exhaustion rules. The trigger check itself causes no Fatigue. The mark ends after the check, expiry, or unsealing. Only one can be armed per target. This replaces a 100 IC check on every action.

## Genjutsu

- **Illusory Wounds** apply their listed penalties without dealing HP damage or triggering an effect that requires a real Wound. Use the strongest matching penalty at the same location. Conditions directly caused by the illusion end with it. Real Major or higher Wounds and Kai retain their normal roles in breaking Direct Genjutsu.
- **Hell Viewing (D)** now inflicts Stun 3 + margin, capped at 8. After the target is first affected that day, use just the margin, capped at 5. Previously its base was 5 on first use, its margin was uncapped, and an empty Willpower pool increased the base. A margin of 10+ can still cost the target 1 Willpower once a day.
- **Brain Destruction (C)** inflicts a Minor illusory Wound, or Major at a margin of 5+. Removed the Severe result at 10+. It still picks the location randomly and replaces a previous instance. **Iron Maiden (B)** keeps its chosen location, Stun, and Major result at 10+. Both now follow the shared rule that an illusory Wound cannot release another Direct Genjutsu.
- **Echo Chamber (B)** retains −4 to hearing-based Awareness for 25 IC. It adds +2 Final Speed to the first defensive Interrupt against a hostile action each IC. Removed Stun 5, +3 Seal Speed, and the +3 defensive surcharge against every incoming hostile action. The victim must hear the initial sound.
- **Drowning Dream (B)** starts at illusory Suffocation 2. Its severity penalizes Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls. Attacking or using handseals raises severity after the action, up to 4. A Speed 6 composure action reduces it by 2 and ends the illusion at zero. Removed Stun 6, Immobilization 4, +2 action Speed, the old maximum of 6, and the extra Stamina check when severity rises. Duration remains 20 IC. Real Suiton damage still helps the victim escape.
- **Killing Intent (B)** caps Stun 5 + margin at 12. Its sight requirement, Paralysis up to 6, and Realization timing remain.
- **Nervous Convulsions (A)** adds 4 IC to a non-Interrupt action of Final Speed 10 or more. Use the original Speed for damage. Each affected action grants a fresh Genjutsu Defense roll afterward. Removed the recurring Paralysis of up to 10 for twice the triggering action’s Speed. Short actions and defenses no longer feed the effect.

The three school pages and search index carry these changes. General Ninjutsu and Genjutsu also gained short introductions explaining their roles.
