# Sage Mode and Senjutsu · 18 November 2026

## Updating your Sage

Add the starting art for your tradition. Keep your chosen art. If you already selected the granted art, spend that selection on another eligible general or tribal choice at your current rank. Neither Core pick counts toward Mastery. Existing ranks, Sage Chakra caps, Upkeep and Perfect Communion progression carry over.

## Shared rules

- A successful Gather gives 3 Sage Chakra outside Mode and 2 in Mode, up to your cap. Flowing Communion spends 1 AP to bring an in-Mode Gather to 3. Speed 10, Delay 8 and the opportunity to interrupt the Sage still apply. Swift Communion uses the current minimum after Mastery.
- An uninterrupted rank-1 opening from empty reaches Mode on IC 10 and the first main-action art on IC 13. Previously those counts were 20 and 23.
- Passive pool, Gather and entry benefits apply during setup. The last point pays for a whole effect using its declared Sage Power. An abort still spends it. Effects tied to Mode end when it does. Only forced excess can cause Natural Imbalance.
- Bonus damage dice use the attack’s base die size, or its smallest base die if mixed. Natural Shock now has a rollable formula, (2 + Sage Power)d8 Blunt + NDB × final Speed. On a full hit, trade the dice for opposed Knockback of up to 2 + Sage Power yards. Senjutsu Infusion’s choice is made before the defence, including for static Accuracy.
- Sage Reversal may swap places with a willing adjacent ally after a successful melee Parry. Earthen Root can anchor an adjacent ally against forced movement using the Sage’s Resistance in place of theirs. Nature Pulse can relay a bearing to a teammate, who still needs their own detection to target someone hidden.

## Traditions

| Tradition and granted art | Changes at the table |
| --- | --- |
| Wild · Nature Pulse | Choose an aspect on entry and change it when an in-Mode Gather resolves. Use Pulse and Survival to find load-bearing footing, trace tracks across a gap or locate a disturbance from active Senjutsu. |
| Great Toad · Pond-Leap | Choose Steady or Springing on entry as well as after Gathering. Spend Springing to combine the leap with a melee Taijutsu attack for +1 Speed. Ripple Strike can threaten Prone from a 2-yard shove or a shove stopped by solid terrain. |
| Serpent · Shed the Binding | Removing the last physical Immobilization can slip you 2 + Power yards away. A full extended-reach melee Taijutsu hit lets you draw yourself adjacent along a clear path. Constricting Coil now has a 10 IC duration. |
| Roc · Updraft Step | Catch a willing falling ally as a Speed 3 Interrupt if your Step can reach them and a landing. Talon Dive supplies at least 5 yards of downward approach from above its target and can land safely. A successful airborne Gather with Hang Upon the Sky grants a brief perch. |
| Tortoise · Still Shell | The first Block in each Gather preserves it, with a concentration check if damage gets through. In Mode, choose an adjacent ally when you prepare to Block or Gather in your shell. Intercept for them at Speed 3 and use your prepared Block. Intercepting costs the Gather. Unbroken Shell can protect one Gather per battle against repeated Blocks and the resulting concentration checks. |
| Moth · Dust Veil | Place a stationary 10 IC veil within 5 yards and feel movement crossing its edge. Powder Mark can share the Search benefit and clear sight through your veil with one ally. Moonwing Drift can Hide you at its endpoint inside the veil against sight-based observers. |
| Carrion · Circle the Wounded | Circle detected quarry before it is hurt. Once injured, follow its voluntary retreat with a Speed 2 Interrupt. Hunt Injury Gates and Open Wounds now count for relevant arts. Scavenger’s Claim can hand an Open Wound to one ally for up to 5 extra IC. Badly Injured or Critical can trigger Scavenger’s Communion, and Last Descent works on Critical hunt monsters. |
| Fungal · Mycelial Ground | Maintain two patches connected across a continuous solid surface. Speak from one to listeners at the other or originate Spore Veil, Hyphal Snare or Nature Pulse there. Snare lasts 10 IC and breaks if the target gets more than 2 yards from its anchor. |
| Salamander · Regrowth | Touch-heal a willing ally and optionally take some of their Burn, Bleed or Poison. An ally can receive Regrowth once per 20 IC across all Sages. Regenerative Balance sheds severity on a completed Rest even when you have no Stamina penalty. Wound effects stay with their owner. |
