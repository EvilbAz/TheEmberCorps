# Elemental jutsu changelog · 11 November 2026

The five basic elements now have 171 learnable techniques. One A-rank Doton technique was retired. Characters who knew Rock Barrage receive Earth Dragon Blast in its place. If they knew both, they recover the XP spent on Rock Barrage.

## Katon · Fire

- **Flame Whorl (B)** became a close-range Ignite finisher. The old version was a Speed 8 parry with an 8d10 exploding-dice retaliation against a nearby attacker and no NDB on that retaliation. The new version is a Speed 12, Area 5 attack centered on the caster, dealing 22d10 plus normal NDB damage. Roll damage once. For each target fully hit, you can spend up to 5 of its Ignite severity for 12 extra damage per point. At 5 Ignite you can trade the ongoing condition for 60 damage now. Chakra 28 and Seal Speed 12 carry over. Fire Tornado remains the parry that retaliates.

## Raiton · Lightning

- **Faraday Cage (C)** costs 18 Chakra with no Upkeep. Its old cost was 16 + 2X with Upkeep X, and repeated casts built a +2 to +8 parry bonus. It now gains +2 against an attacker with Raiton-induced Paralysis. A full parry lets the caster move up to 3 yards after resolution. Moving or using another technique no longer costs accumulated stacks.
- **Dampening Field (C)** jams electronics and magnetized compasses and gives −10 on chakra-sensing checks to detect creatures in the field, including allies’ checks. The caster must stay stationary but can act and can end the field by dropping Upkeep. Removed Immobility 10, the separate Speed 10 / Delay 5 shutdown, the 50% reduction to Raiton damage, the 40% reduction to Doton damage, and the +6 surcharge on Doton costs and Upkeep.
- **Raikiri (A)** now deals 50d10, down from 52d10. Accuracy goes from −2 to +1, Speed from 18 to 12, and Seal Speed from 22 to 14. It ignores 50% of DR and Armor effectiveness, up from 25% each. Removed the +2 Chakra penalty after use. It still requires Chidori and inflicts Paralysis 4 for 25 IC. Chidori rewards a run-up and time spent charging. Raikiri gives an armored target a much shorter opening.

## Suiton · Water

- **Water Formation Pillar (C)** now uses the name *Suijinchū*. *Suijinheki* refers to the B-rank Water Encampment Wall. The pillar protects its user on a full parry and still supplies 10 gallons on success. Removed the group-protection clause that referred to an Area the pillar did not have. Its existing bookmark still works.
- **Water Dragon Blast (B)** changed from 20d6 + 10G + 15 to 16d6 + 5G + 15. Stun 10 became Knockback equal to half the invested gallons, rounded down. At 10–20 gallons that’s 5–10 yards.
- **Great Waterfall (A)** changed from 10G + 30 to 6G + 30. Removed Stun 14. The wave still pushes targets 20–40 yards down its line according to gallons invested.
- **Great Shark Blast (A)** changed from 18d20 + 8G + 20 to 16d20 + 5G + 20. When intercepting a projectile, add 5G solely to the comparison. A tie ends both attacks. If the shark wins, use its original damage roll against the attacker. Removed the rule that inflicted full damage whenever a ninjutsu defense fell short. The extra gallons buy a stronger counter without adding the same bonus to the return hit.

## Doton · Earth

- **Mud Flow Deflect (C)** explicitly protects one creature, including yourself, within 16 yards. The incoming attack must originate more than 5 yards from the caster. Corrected the copied Earth Shore Return reference and stray weapon text. The +3 parry bonus and rank limits carry over.
- **Earth Dragon Blast (A)** changed from 64d10 to 48d10. Removed its special reduction to Blocking. It still inflicts Stun 10. On a full hit against a grounded target, the ground within 3 yards of that target becomes Rocky Terrain 3 for 20 IC.
- **Rock Barrage (A)** was retired. Earth Dragon Blast fills the ranged blunt-attack slot and gives its user a way to hold ground. Existing links point to the replacement or its conversion note.
- **Mountain Earth (A)** changed from 150d6 to 90d6. Removed the parry prohibition, doubled damage for Wounds, and cascading extra Wounds. The collision now inflicts Stun 12 and leaves Rocky Terrain 4 across the Area for 40 IC. Its −10 Accuracy, 30 IC Delay, and Area 80 still give both teams time and reason to move.

## Fūton · Wind

- **Wind Turtle’s Shell (C)** can shield one ally within 5 yards as an alternative to shielding yourself. Its ranged parry bonuses and rank limits carry over.
- **Vacuum Sphere (B)** changed from 11d6 × 5 to 9d6 × 5. Removed +75% Wounding and the damage that could pass through someone using Take the Hit. A full hit now lets the caster push each victim up to 5 yards away, choosing the distance separately.
- **Vacuum Great Sphere (A)** changed from 20d8 × 5 to 14d8 × 5. Removed Stun 10, the automatic Wound category increase, and the special denial of partial defense. A full hit pushes the target to the far end of the 30-yard line, stopping at obstacles.
- **Blade of Wind (A)** can be Blocked under the normal Wind rule. Removed its 50% DR bypass and +50% Wounding. It retains +4 Accuracy, half damage bonus, and consecutive attacks after a hit.
- **Scything Wind (A)** changed from 7d10 × 8 to 5d10 × 8. It now picks up to three targets within 40 yards and uses one Accuracy roll and one damage roll. Each target defends against a Projectile. Removed unlimited targeting, the effective Area 40 for Dodge, and +50% Wounding. Each full hit can push its target up to 5 yards away.
- **Renkuudan (A)** caps Knockback at 30 yards. Its damage, range, and charge duration still scale past 30 IC. A target moved by a full hit at 25 or more charged IC still falls Prone.
