# Ember Corps rules pass · 18 November 2026

## Balance and rules decisions

| Rule | Previous text or result | Published ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analyst | Reduced a Downtime Research TN by Research ranks + 10 while adding those same ranks to the check. At Research 15, TN 35 became TN 10 before rolling 1d20 + 15. | Flat −5 TN on Downtime Research. That example now needs a 15 on the die against TN 30. Analyst keeps its Research ranks and rank-cap increase. The ruling follows Analyst through an upgrade that retains it. |
| Vanishing Point | A successful Hide granted movement and another legal Speed 0 Hide. Already-hidden characters can Hide again, triggering more movement on the same count. | The Hide granted by Vanishing Point cannot trigger Vanishing Point. A full Dodge or ordinary successful Hide still grants the movement and follow-up. |
| South Star Combo | Its A-rank entry capped Paralysis at 6 but omitted the Cadence that inflicted it. | A full hit adds 1 Paralysis per CC held at declaration, up to the existing single-source cap of 6. At CC 0 it adds none. At CC 2 it adds 2. After the combo ends, lose 1 severity every 10 IC. This is a new Cadence. |
| Roc Sage | An unsupported Updraft Step started falling immediately. At Sage Power 1 its 10-yard ascent could be gone one IC into the Speed 3 action. | While in Sage Mode, Step can hold its endpoint for up to 3 IC so you can declare your next main action there. Movement, forced movement or losing Sage Mode ends it early. From suspension, Talon Dive can count the descent already available through Move with an Attack toward its 5-yard requirement. The attack must still reach. |
| Contract Seal | Dismissed summons through a contract. Ember’s Beast Seal expressly grants no contract, leaving the interaction with a deployed Tamed Monster unresolved. | A full hit on the handler can recall their deployed monster. The existing lock lasts 30 IC or until unsealed. Recall preserves Vitality, statuses and limited-use effects. The existing contract use remains available. |

## Rulings where two passages disagreed

| Topic | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Blocking and Called Shots | The Wound reference let the blocker choose a location against a Called Shot. Core listed Called Shots as an exception. Both now give the blocker the choice against an ordinary Called Shot. A technique that fixes the location itself keeps it. |
| Wounds without an explicit damage tag | The Jutsu reader’s guide previously prevented any untyped technique from Wounding. It now applies the Wound chapter’s existing unarmed, elemental and weapon defaults first. An attack with neither a printed nor a default type still cannot Wound. |
| AoE Dodge | The compendium’s prose could use the nearest edge, while Core and the shape table used the listed dimensions. Both use the radius, shorter Line dimension or Cube side. Each yard of movement short during the attack’s final Speed gives −2 to Dodge. |
| Grapple Control | Replaced “reduced to that amount” with a worked transfer of Control across zero. A printed Control cost is paid before effects. The unlisted cost of one-third Speed, rounded down, is applied after the action, including Interrupts. The cap, Wound losses and roll formulas are preserved. |
| Pin, Guard, Mount and Weapon Pin | “Speed reduced to 0” now explicitly means movement speed. A pinned Hunter still pays action Speed, including for Escape Pin. |
| Reversal | The prerequisite named Escape Pin, but the retroactive clause also named Break Clinch. Both now require Escape Pin. Its printed Speed, Delay and retroactive Stamina cost still apply. |
| First Aid Kit | A Wound’s specific treatment Speed takes precedence over the kit’s 15 Speed per category. For example, a Wound that specifies Speed 25 and a Medicine check uses both. |
| Slow Roasted | The Critical Wound recovered 1 WP per OOC day but described the ordinary refresh as daily. It now explicitly overrides the ordinary scene refresh while the Wound persists. |
| Exhaustion and Partial Success | The guide and the two cost-check summaries now point to the full Fatigue failure scale, including failure past incapacitation. Ordinary Partial Success tops out at the full attack effect. A miss by more than five cannot accidentally amplify incoming damage. Special adjustments still apply. |

## Reference and wording

- **Core combat and the New Player Guide.** Shortened the combat introduction and linked directly to initiative, actions, interrupts, hiding and searching. The attack declaration now includes the target and timing. Replaced the fixed 2d4 unarmed example with the published XP formula and a 500 XP example (3d4 plus PDB × 8). Corrected the combat example to use modified Seal Speed and Prepare Blocking. Replaced named jutsu with stale costs in the Fatigue example with a worked example tracking the two penalties separately. Condensed the four legal ways to Hide.
- **Team Attacks and hunts.** Clarified that all two to four contributors are named before preparation earns credit. Added worked IC examples for mounting a monster with Grab and for a capture attempt whose restraint expires before the Delay completes.
- **Grapple Taijutsu.** Removed obsolete sheet cell references from the Offense and Defense formulas. Corrected the spelling of Shunshin, the Severe Wound label and spacing in Guillotine Choke.
- **Clans.** Fixed the broken link inside Gentle Fist’s +4 Speed modifier and clarified Vitality overflow and the existing HP Wound comparison. Electromagnetic Dominance now prints its shared Chakra 10 cost in a field visible in the technique finder.
- **Medical jutsu and injuries.** Corrected Wound Sealing Technique’s Vitality wording. Temporary Suture now assigns its existing extra week per severity to each treated Wound’s own recovery clock. Removed two paragraphs of flavour before Slow Roasted’s mechanical effects.
- **Equipment.** Cut retired crafting-tier terminology. Condensed Blank Scroll, Rope, Compass, Map, Flashlight and Oxygen Tank entries. The wilderness navigation bonus with both compass and map is +4 total. The tank entry now states its existing price of 5,000 Ryō and its 30 minutes of air cleanly.
- **Cinder Chases.** Added yard equivalents for the existing 5 m and 15 m interception distances when switching back to combat.
- **Technique finder and navigation.** Removed Body Check’s D-rank badge and finder row. Its old link still leads to Shove, the existing free basic action. Restored the old Summoning Contracts anchor beside the Beast Seal rules. Sage links from the directory, search and internal navigation now open the relevant tradition. Rebuilt both indexes and added a regression test for landing inside a collapsed tradition.
