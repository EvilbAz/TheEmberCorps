At Haven Ember’s smokehouse, every parcel carries two marks. The first tells the cook what’s inside: collar, haunch, claw or coil. The second is the butcher’s note on the beast. A tortoise that spent its life bracing against the river gives a different stew from one that hauled prey beneath the bank.

## Cook for the Team
{:#cook-for-the-team}

Before the encounter, **one hunter spends 1 Downtime Slot** to cook for the Hunting Team. A single batch feeds every attending team member, including the cook (up to four). Choose one market vegetable and, if available, one monster cut. Spend the ingredients when you cook. A fire and ordinary camp utensils are enough.

Roll **1d20 + Survival or Lore ranks** once for the whole pot. Add the bonus from one piece of cookware if its condition applies. Each hunter who eats gets the result below for that Hunt’s encounter. Each diner may benefit from one meal per Hunt. Eating is optional.

| Cooking total | What each diner gets |
|---|---|
| 14 or less · Camp fare | One use of the vegetable effect. |
| 15–24 · Well cooked | One use of the vegetable effect and one use of either meat effect. The diner chooses the meat effect when its trigger occurs. |
| 25+ · House special | One use each of the vegetable, Tribe and Combat Class effects. |

**Vegetable-only meals:** On 15 or higher, each diner may use the vegetable effect twice, on separate actions. All uses belong to the individual diner and expire at the end of the encounter. Declare a roll bonus before rolling. Cooking grants the meal result directly and earns no Preparation points.

## Bring Meat Home
{:#bring-meat-home}

When the Team brings back a slain Hunt target with edible remains, take **one cut for the Team** alongside its normal rewards. One hunter may roll **Survival or Lore against TN 15** while dressing it; success yields a second cut. Make one harvest roll per target. Dressing the carcass is part of the Hunt reward and costs no Downtime.

For a **live capture**, the handler issues one smokehouse chit. Exchange it in Haven Ember for one stored cut of the captured target’s Tribe and Combat Class. For a drive-off, the Team may take one cut only if it recovered an edible severed part during the encounter. When a target has no edible tissue, the GM identifies that in its Hunt profile; a live-capture chit can instead be exchanged for a cut of the Team’s choice from the smokehouse.

Record **quantity, Tribe and Combat Class** on one hunter’s inventory or the Team’s stores: for example, *2 Mire collars — Grappler*. One cut feeds one whole meal. Salted or sealed cuts keep between Hunts until spent. If a profile lists multiple classes, the GM names the one that shaped the cut when awarding it.

## Tribe Cuts
{:#tribe-cuts}

| Tribe | Cut | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Fang | Runner’s flank | When you move to close the distance to a visible monster, add 5 yards to that movement. |
| Colossus | Marbled haunch | When an effect would move you against your will, reduce that displacement by up to 5 yards. |
| Chitin | Shellback oyster | When you take a physical hit while Blocking, reduce the damage remaining after Block by 10, to a minimum of 0. |
| Sky | Wing muscle | When you aim at a monster above you, add 2 Accuracy to one ranged attack against it. |
| Coil | Ribbon loin | When something holds you, add 3 to your Break Clinch or Escape Pin roll. |
| Mire | River collar | When you make a Stamina roll while swimming, wading or standing in mud, add 3 to the roll. |
| Colony | Brood pearls | When another hunter within 5 yards rolls to resist a status, give that hunter +2. Only one diner can assist a given roll this way. |

## Combat Class Effects
{:#class-effects}

The cut’s second mark tells the cook how to handle it. Apply the matching row to the same cut.

| Combat Class | Butcher’s note | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Bruiser | Sear the thick grain | On a full melee hit against the monster, add 1d10 damage of the attack’s type. |
| Skirmisher | Slice across the lean | When you Dodge a monster that moved as part of its attack, add 2 to your Dodge roll. |
| Controller | Simmer the silver seams | When you finish setting a trap during the encounter, add 2 to its Trap TN for that placement. |
| Artillery | Roast the long fibres | When you declare a ranged attack, increase its maximum Range by 10 yards for that attack. |
| Ambusher | Smoke the dark meat | When you use Search to locate a hidden monster, add 3 to your Awareness roll. |
| Grappler | Braise the knuckles | When you use Secure Grip while Clinging, add 3 to your Grapple Offense roll. |
| Tank | Stew the bone end | When a monster deals damage to armor you are wearing, reduce that Durability loss by 10, to a minimum of 0. |
| Swarm | Toast the small morsels | When a teammate also lies within an Area attack aimed at your Team, add 2 to your own defense roll against it. |

## Market Vegetables and Cookware
{:#market-ingredients}

Haven Ember’s produce stalls sell vegetables by the bundle. Each price buys enough for the entire pot. The cookware prices buy reusable equipment. [Browse the illustrated kitchen stock.]({{ '/rules/standard-items/#items-hunt-kitchen' | relative_url }})

#### Ember Radishes
{:#ember-radishes}

**200 Ryō** · When a monster inflicts Burn on you, reduce its Severity by 1, to a minimum of 0.

#### Lantern Leeks
{:#lantern-leeks}

**250 Ryō** · When you roll Chakra Exhaustion for a Ninjutsu or Genjutsu, add 3 to the roll.

#### Stonecap Mushrooms
{:#stonecap-mushrooms}

**200 Ryō** · When a monster hits you while you are Prone, reduce the damage you take by 8, to a minimum of 0.

#### Field Peas
{:#field-peas}

**150 Ryō** · When you use a First Aid Kit on a teammate, reduce that action’s Speed by 2, to a minimum of 3.

#### Iron Stewpot
{:#iron-stewpot}

**2,000 Ryō** · +2 Cooking when braising a Colossus, Chitin or Mire cut, or making a vegetable-only stew.

#### Folding Grill
{:#folding-grill}

**2,000 Ryō** · +2 Cooking when grilling a Fang, Sky, Coil or Colony cut.

#### Butcher’s Roll
{:#butchers-roll}

**1,500 Ryō** · +2 to the harvest roll when dressing a slain target with edible remains.

## From the Hunt Log
{:#sample-meal}

A four-hunter Team brings a Mire Grappler home alive and trades its chit for a collar. Before the next Hunt, the cook buys Lantern Leeks for 250 Ryō and spends one Downtime Slot braising the collar. She rolls 13, adds 7 Survival and 2 for the Iron Stewpot: **22, well cooked**.

Each hunter gets one leek use and one meat use. The cook saves her leeks for a Chakra Exhaustion roll. The climber spends his meat use on Secure Grip. The scout keeps hers for a Stamina roll in the marsh.
