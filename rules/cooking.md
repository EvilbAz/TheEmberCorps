At Haven Ember’s smokehouse, every parcel carries two marks. The first tells the cook what’s inside: collar, haunch, claw or coil. The second is the butcher’s note on the beast. A tortoise that spent its life bracing against the river gives a different stew from one that hauled prey beneath the bank.

## Cook for the Team
{:#cook-for-the-team}

Before the encounter, **one hunter spends 1 Downtime Slot** to cook for the Hunting Team. A single batch feeds every attending team member, including the cook (up to four). Choose one market vegetable and, if available, one monster cut. Spend the ingredients when you cook. A fire and ordinary camp utensils are enough.

Roll **1d20 + Survival or Lore ranks** once for the whole pot. Add the bonus from one piece of cookware if its condition applies. Each hunter who eats gets the result below for that Hunt’s encounter. Each diner may benefit from one meal per Hunt. Eating is optional.

| Cooking total | What each diner gets |
|---|---|
| 14 or less · Camp fare | Gain the vegetable benefit for the encounter. |
| 15–24 · Well cooked | Gain the vegetable benefit. Each diner also chooses the cut’s Tribe or Combat Class benefit before the encounter. |
| 25+ · House special | Gain the vegetable, Tribe and Combat Class benefits together. |

Meal benefits last throughout the encounter. Apply percentage changes to the listed quantity, rounding the change down (minimum 1 for a positive quantity). Add meal percentages that modify the same quantity before rounding. A meal cannot reduce a positive cost below 1. Declare any reroll after seeing your die and use the higher result; a die can be rerolled by food only once.

**Vegetable-only meals:** On 15 or higher, each diner also gets one reroll on any d20 check they make during the encounter. On 25 or higher, each gets two. The vegetable benefit lasts the whole encounter at every quality.

## Bring Meat Home
{:#bring-meat-home}

When the Team brings back a slain Hunt target with edible remains, take **one cut for the Team** alongside its normal rewards. One hunter may roll **Survival or Lore against TN 15** while dressing it; success yields a second cut. Make one harvest roll per target. Dressing the carcass is part of the Hunt reward and costs no Downtime.

For a **live capture**, the handler issues one smokehouse chit. Exchange it in Haven Ember for one stored cut of the captured target’s Tribe and Combat Class. For a drive-off, the Team may take one cut only if it recovered an edible severed part during the encounter. When a target has no edible tissue, the GM identifies that in its Hunt profile; a live-capture chit can instead be exchanged for a cut of the Team’s choice from the smokehouse.

Record **quantity, Tribe and Combat Class** on one hunter’s inventory or the Team’s stores: for example, *2 Mire collars — Grappler*. One cut feeds one whole meal. Salted or sealed cuts keep between Hunts until spent. If a profile lists multiple classes, the GM names the one that shaped the cut when awarding it.

## Tribe Cuts
{:#tribe-cuts}

| Tribe | Cut | Meal benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Fang | Runner’s flank | Increase the distance you can move with your own movement by 25%. |
| Colossus | Marbled haunch | Start the encounter with temporary Vitality equal to 15% of your maximum Vitality. Lose it before ordinary Vitality. It cannot be replenished and expires after the encounter. |
| Chitin | Shellback oyster | Reduce incoming attack damage by 10% at the percentage-reduction step. |
| Sky | Wing muscle | Gain +2 Accuracy on attacks, including attacks that use rolled Accuracy. |
| Coil | Ribbon loin | Gain +2 to Dodge and to all Grapple checks, including checks made while Clinging. |
| Mire | River collar | Reduce the Stamina and Chakra costs of your actions by 10%. |
| Colony | Brood pearls | Once per encounter, when you or a teammate you can see loses Vitality, restore 10% of that hunter’s maximum Vitality after the hit. One diner can answer each hit. Recovery cannot exceed normal maximum Vitality. |

## Combat Class Effects
{:#class-effects}

The cut’s second mark tells the cook how to handle it. Apply the matching row to the same cut.

| Combat Class | Butcher’s note | Meal benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Bruiser | Sear the thick grain | Your damaging attacks deal 10% more damage before mitigation. |
| Skirmisher | Slice across the lean | Gain +2 to defense rolls and increase your own movement distance by 10%. |
| Controller | Simmer the silver seams | Increase by 2 any TN you set for an opponent to resist a status, escape a restraint or resist a Genjutsu. This includes traps you set. |
| Artillery | Roast the long fibres | Increase the listed Range of your ranged attacks and techniques by 25%. Touch and Self retain their usual reach. |
| Ambusher | Smoke the dark meat | Gain +2 Accuracy and +2 on Stealth and Search checks. |
| Grappler | Braise the knuckles | Roll twice and keep the higher die on Athletics and Grapple checks, including Cling and escape attempts. |
| Tank | Stew the bone end | Reduce damage taken by 15% when determining Wounds. Gain +2 on Resistance rolls. |
| Swarm | Toast the small morsels | When you hit a monster, the next teammate to attack it gains +2 Accuracy on that attack. Further hits refresh the bonus. A single attack can receive this bonus from only one diner. |

## Market Vegetables and Cookware

#### Lantern Leeks

**250 Ryō** · Reduce the Chakra cost of your actions by 10% and gain +2 on Chakra Exhaustion rolls.

#### Stonecap Mushrooms
{:#stonecap-mushrooms}

#### Field Peas
{:#field-peas}

{:#iron-stewpot}

{:#folding-grill}

#### Butcher’s Roll
{:#butchers-roll}

**1,500 Ryō** · +2 to the harvest roll when dressing a slain target with edible remains.

## From the Hunt Log
{:#sample-meal}

A four-hunter Team brings a Mire Grappler home alive and trades its chit for a collar. Before the next Hunt, the cook buys Lantern Leeks for 250 Ryō and spends one Downtime Slot braising the collar. She rolls 13, adds 7 Survival and 2 for the Iron Stewpot: **22, well cooked**.

Everyone pays 10% less Chakra and gains +2 on Chakra Exhaustion rolls throughout the encounter. The climber chooses Grappler and rolls twice on Grapple checks. The medic chooses Mire: a 40-Chakra technique now costs 32, and a 20-Stamina action costs 18.
