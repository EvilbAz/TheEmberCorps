## Class Benefits
{:#class-benefits}

Hunter Classes are Corps specialties. To join one, complete its qualification tasks with staff and record approval on your character sheet. Staff provide the current qualification requirements through the server’s class application process. Your class benefits begin when the application is approved.

## Hunter Classes
{:#hunter-classes}

#### Pathfinder
{:#pathfinder}

Scouts, trackers and navigators who find routes through the Wastes.

##### Always a Way
{:#always-a-way}

Gain +3 Survival and Awareness when tracking, navigating, locating something or identifying environmental hazards.

Once per Hunt or Mission, after you or an ally fails a navigation, tracking, search or environmental-hazard check, that character may reroll it with +5 and must keep the new result. On success, ask the GM for one relevant environmental detail, such as a safer route, shelter, signs of passage or unstable terrain.

#### Slayer
{:#slayer}

Hunters trained to bring down dangerous creatures.

##### Bring it Down
{:#bring-it-down}

Treat damage you deal as 25% higher for Wound determination only.

#### Anchor
{:#anchor}

Defenders who protect crews, caravans and settlements.

##### Nobody Gets Through
{:#nobody-gets-through}

When an ally within 3 yards is attacked, spend 1 Willpower to use Take the Hit at its normal Speed without a roll or distance penalty. You must be able to reach the ally through a legal path and meet Take the Hit’s other restrictions. On success, you become the attack’s target; defend and resolve area attacks using the normal Take the Hit rules.

After successfully defending against an attack or taking one for an ally, you may Hold the Line. Gain RES/2 DR and +5 to Defensive rolls until after your next non-Interrupt Action. These benefits apply after the triggering attack resolves. Further activations refresh the duration; they do not stack.

#### Trapper
{:#trapper}

Specialists in snares, seals, explosives, bait and ambushes.

Whenever you successfully complete Prepare during a Hunt, create one additional Preparation, subject to the Hunt’s normal Preparation limit.

Once per battle, reveal one preparation you could have established before the encounter. Describe its location and purpose when revealed; the GM must confirm that it was feasible. It grants one of the following:

- +5 to one relevant Skill check.
- +3 Accuracy to one attack that exploits it.
- A snare: make a Survival Status Roll against the Resistance of one creature triggering it. On a failed defence, apply Immobilization 5 for 20 IC.
- Reduce one environmental or enemy-created numerical penalty by 5 for you and allies within 3 yards, for 20 IC. This does not remove the underlying effect.

Pay the normal costs of any equipment used. This cannot supply rare items or change events that have already resolved.

#### Scavenger
{:#scavenger}

Salvagers who recover useful equipment and monster materials.

After a Hunt, Mission or expedition with recoverable salvage, identify one additional usable Salvage or Trophy component. The GM selects a component appropriate to what remains; it must still be recovered normally.

Once per Week’s Rest, convert expendable salvage worth at least 2,500 Ryō into one Field Supply. You may keep one at a time. Outside combat, spend it to assemble one ordinary consumable or tool worth up to 2,500 Ryō that can reasonably be made from those materials. It cannot provide weapons, armour, explosives or rare items.

Alternatively, expend it before rolling to gain +5 on one Prepare, Artisan, Survival or Medicine check during a Hunt.

#### Chirurgeon
{:#chirurgeon}

Medics and anatomists trained to treat Hunters and study beasts.

Gain +3 Medicine on checks to treat Wounds or Stabilise an injured character.

Once per battle, use Emergency Field Treatment **[Speed 10, Utility, Touch]** on a willing or helpless living creature with a Severe or Critical Wound, or at 0 HP or lower. It cannot treat a dead creature. On resolution:

- If below 1 HP, restore it to 1 HP.
- End one non-permanent injury-caused Bleed, Burn or Stun.
- Suppress the numerical penalties of one Wound until the battle ends. The Wound remains; missing or unusable body parts remain unusable.

Using this treatment on yourself costs 1 Willpower and requires you to be conscious and able to act.

#### Hound
{:#hound}

Tracking and pursuit specialists.

Increase Movement Speed by 50% while pursuing a target you can reliably locate through sight, tracking, scent or another sense. This increases distance travelled, not Action Speed.

Gain +3 Survival and Awareness to locate or pursue creatures you have previously encountered.

#### Breaker
{:#breaker}

Hunters trained to breach armour, barriers and fortifications.

Your attacks ignore 2 DR. Treat damage dealt to armour, barriers, structures and other defensive objects as 25% higher for durability loss only.

Once per battle, declare Crack It Open after a hit is confirmed but before damage is rolled. That attack ignores 50% of the target’s DR instead of the flat 2, and its damage against armour, barriers, objects and structures is treated as 50% higher instead of 25%. If it Wounds a creature, you may choose the location from the legal results for that damage type. The defender retains its normal Wound mitigation.
