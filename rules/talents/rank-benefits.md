## Rank Benefits
{:#rank-benefits}

Corps ranks reflect training, responsibility and service to Haven Ember. Strays gain no Rank Benefits. On reaching Trailhand, Huntsman, Warden or Legend, gain that rank’s Automatic Benefit and choose one benefit from that rank or a lower rank. Each benefit may be selected once unless stated otherwise.

A Talent that grants a Rank Benefit teaches the selected ability; it does not confer the rank, office or authority associated with it.

### Stray
{:#stray}

Strays train under veteran Hunters and have not yet earned Rank Benefits.

### Trailhand
{:#trailhand}

Trailhands are trusted to take assignments beyond Ember’s walls without constant supervision.

#### Take Point — Automatic
{:#take-point-automatic}

When you are the designated leader of a group, you may give a specific order to another Hunter up to 3 + XP/1000 times per in-character week.

They gain +3 to one relevant roll made directly to carry out that order. If applicable, an ordered attack instead gains +3 Accuracy.

Orders must be specific. "Get them out of there," "Hold that door," or "Pin its left leg" qualify. "Do your best" does not. If commanding an ally to take an attack, it must be specific to a subtype. You cannot just say “attack that enemy” - it must be, at minimum “Use a Katon Damaging Area attack on that enemy” for example.

#### Something to Come Home To
{:#something-to-come-home-to}

Choose a promise, principle, person, or purpose that keeps you moving when you should have stopped. The GMs must approve your choice.

Once every 7 OOC days, while directly acting in accordance with it, you may reset your Chakra Exhaustion and Stamina penalties to 0 and reduce your Fatigue by 1.

If you knowingly betray that principle, you cannot use this Benefit for 14 OOC days.

#### Too Stubborn to Quit
{:#too-stubborn-to-quit}

You may spend multiple points of Willpower, activations of Limit Break, or similar effects on the same roll, limited only by what you have available.

#### Bring Them Home
{:#bring-them-home}

Whenever an ally is Severely Wounded, Critically Wounded, or reduced to 0 HP, you may trigger this Benefit. Until after your next non-Interrupt Action, you ignore penalties from Fatigue and non-damaging Status Effects. Any Genjutsu currently affecting you is purged, and you gain +5 to defensive Genjutsu rolls during this time.

Multiple triggers overlap, but do not stack.

#### Wasteland Conditioning
{:#wasteland-conditioning}

Whenever you Rest, reduce your Stamina penalty by 1 additional point and reduce Suffocation by 1. You may also spend Willpower to reroll a Fatigue Check. Doing so causes you to succeed, making the result of your dice the exact number you needed to pass, even if that number is above 20.

#### Trailwise
{:#trailwise}

Once per Hunt, when you fail a Track, Research, Study Weakness or Prepare roll, you may reroll it. You must keep the new result.

#### Hard Country
{:#hard-country}

As a Speed 0 Action, you may activate or deactivate this Benefit. While active, halve the numerical penalties caused by Fatigue and non-damaging Status Effects.

You may maintain this for a total of 50 IC per battle. These ICs do not need to be consecutive.

### Huntsman
{:#huntsman}

Huntsmen lead expeditions, patrol Ember’s outer territory and train Strays.

#### Pass It Down — Automatic
{:#pass-it-down-automatic}

You may formally train other Hunters in Jutsu you know, allowing them to learn techniques one rank beyond their normal imitation limit. Training takes one week per rank of the technique.

You may also teach eligible Capability Talents you possess. This requires one month of training; the student gains the Talent immediately, but their next Talent must be spent paying for it. A character may only owe one Talent at a time.

#### Second Nature
{:#second-nature}

If your DEX and always-active Uniques alone could reduce the Seal Speed of a Ninjutsu you know to 0, you may perform it without handseals.

This does not apply to Genjutsu, Medical Ninjutsu or Sealing Ninjutsu.

#### Long Haul
{:#long-haul}

Your bonus to Chakra Exhaustion and Stamina rolls from Fatigue increases by +2 per Fatigue category.

#### Call the Hunt
{:#call-the-hunt}

As a Speed 10 Action that cannot be reduced by AP, you may call out up to two Orders. Allies may choose whether to obey. These benefits last 50 IC, or until you issue another order this way.

Fallback: +3 to Defensive rolls while withdrawing. An ally making a hostile Action loses this Order’s benefit until Call the Hunt ends.

Mark It: +2 Accuracy on the next attack against a target within 3 yards of you.

Regroup: +25% Movement while moving toward you or a point within 3 yards of you.

Hold: Gain RES/2 Damage Reduction while within 3 yards of you.

#### Know What You're Killing
{:#know-what-you-re-killing}

After your Hunting Team successfully completes Study Weakness against a creature, you gain +2 Accuracy against that creature for the resulting Hunt. Your damage against it is also treated as +25% higher for determining Wounds.

#### Take the Hit
{:#take-the-hit}

Once per fight, after seeing the damage of an attack against you, you may spend 1 Willpower to halve the final damage taken.

#### Pack Hunter
{:#pack-hunter}

When an ally successfully damages an enemy, your next attack against that same enemy gains +2 Accuracy. This cannot trigger from your own attacks and does not stack with itself.

### Warden
{:#warden}

Wardens oversee work vital to the Haven: research, medicine, supplies, engineering, archives and reconnaissance.

#### A Useful Trade — Automatic
{:#a-useful-trade-automatic}

Choose one Skill appropriate to your work as a Warden. You gain Specialization in that Skill if you did not already possess it.

Your maximum ranks in that Skill increase by 3. When its natural d20 result is below 10, treat the die as a 10.

#### Practiced Hands
{:#practiced-hands}

Whenever you spend Willpower to reroll a Skill Roll, add +5 to the final result. You also possess 1 temporary Willpower which can only be used to reroll Skill Rolls and recovers normally.

#### Quartermaster
{:#quartermaster}

Whenever you purchase ordinary consumable equipment or field supplies, you receive twice the normal quantity for the same price.

This does not apply to Exploding Tags, permanent equipment, weapons, armor or unique items.

#### Expedition Planner
{:#expedition-planner}

Once per Hunt, when a member of your Hunting Team fails a Track, Research, Study Weakness or Prepare roll, you may order a reroll with +3. The new result must be kept.

#### Field Notes
{:#field-notes}

Once per Hunt, when you personally succeed on Research or Study Weakness, generate 1 additional Intel of the same type.

#### Keep Them Working
{:#keep-them-working}

As a Speed 5 Action, you may end one instance of Stun or Paralysis affecting an ally you can reach and assist.

#### Contingency
{:#contingency}

Once per battle, after you fail a non-Attack Skill Roll, Fatigue Check, or defensive roll against an environmental hazard, you may declare a contingency and reroll it with +5. You must keep the second result.

### Legend
{:#legend}

Legends are Hunters whose deeds have become part of Haven Ember’s history.

#### A Name That Carries — Automatic
{:#a-name-that-carries-automatic}

Within Haven Ember and settlements familiar with its Hunters, your identity is common knowledge.

When your reputation would reasonably help you, gain +5 to social Skill Rolls involving authority, trust or intimidation. People may not like you. They have still heard the stories.

#### First to Move
{:#first-to-move}

Regardless of your Initiative roll, your first Action in combat always occurs on IC 0.

#### One Clean Hit
{:#one-clean-hit}

Once per battle, when Partial Defense would apply against your attack, you may spend 1 Willpower to ignore all effects of Partial Defense for that attack. Declare this before rolling damage.

If the target subsequently avoids the attack entirely, the Willpower is not spent.

#### Marked Quarry
{:#marked-quarry}

Choose one person or creature as your Quarry after spending significant time studying them.

You gain +6 Survival to track them, +5 Awareness and Espionage against them, +3 Accuracy against them, and +25% Damage for determining Wounds against them.

While fighting your Quarry, you gain 1 temporary Willpower, and another every 50 IC. You may only have one Quarry at a time. It takes a Downtime slot to change quarry.

#### Perfect Ambush
{:#perfect-ambush}

When attacking someone completely unaware of your presence, your attack gains +4 Accuracy and +25% Damage. You also prepare Sneak Attacks in half the normal required time.

This cannot be used simply by hiding from somebody who already knows they are fighting you.

#### Still Standing
{:#still-standing}

Once per fight, after taking damage, you may spend 1 Willpower to halve the final damage taken. If this would still reduce you to 0 HP, you may instead remain at 1 HP.

Using the second effect exhausts this Benefit for the remainder of the fight.

#### Against the Impossible
{:#against-the-impossible}

When entering battle against a Dread-class or greater Beast, gain 1 temporary Willpower. Every 50 IC you remain actively engaged with that creature, regain that temporary Willpower if it has been spent.

#### Living Myth
{:#living-myth}

When you spend a point of Willpower, choose one ally who can see or hear you. That ally gains 1 temporary Willpower which lasts until the end of the battle.

A character can only benefit from your Living Myth once per battle.
