# Skill Actions
{:#skill-actions}

Use these actions when a skill gives your Hunter a way to help. Say what you do and what you hope to accomplish. Anyone can warn a friend, offer someone a hand, or talk to a guard. Roll when the outcome is uncertain and failure would matter.

### Making a check
{:#skill-action-scaling}

Roll 1d20 + the named skill against the listed TN. On an opposed check, beat the other creature’s total. When a scene already gives a TN for the same task, use that TN. A new tool, clue or approach can justify another attempt after a failure.

### Timing and assistance
{:#skill-interrupts-and-links}

In combat these are main Utility Actions unless an entry says otherwise. Pay the listed Speed even on a failure. Outside combat, play out the time the task takes. A Hunter who learns something can share it with the party. A warning can change what an ally decides to do. The ally still resolves their own action normally.

## Athletics
{:#athletics-actions}

**Vault — Speed 4, Movement.** With a reachable handhold or landing, roll Athletics against TN 15 to clear a gap or up to 5 yards of difficult ground, or climb up to 3 yards. On a failure you stay where you started.

**Help Up — Speed 4, Touch.** Offer a willing adjacent Prone ally a hand. On Athletics TN 15, they stand in their space. If they are held down, free them first.

## Awareness
{:#awareness-actions}

**Read the Windup — Speed 4.** Watch a visible opponent preparing a move during Delay. On Awareness against 10 + their Espionage, the GM describes one physical tell you could see, such as which hand bears their weight or where they are aiming. You can call it out in time for an ally to act on their turn.

**Keep Watch — Speed 4.** While an ally works, pick one approach you can see or hear. On Awareness TN 15, the GM tells you the first ordinary sign of someone coming that way while you keep watch. A hidden approach uses [Search and Point Out]({{ '/rules/core/#search' | relative_url }}).

## Chakra Control
{:#chakra-control-actions}

**Steady Hand — Speed 4, Touch.** Feed a small, steady trickle of chakra into a willing partner’s practice exercise or a tool designed to accept it. On Chakra Control TN 15, hold the output steady long enough for your partner to trace a fault or demonstrate the exercise. Activating a technique still uses its printed costs and requirements.

**Trace the Break — Speed 5, Touch.** Test an accessible chakra line on a training rig, lamp or similar device. On Chakra Control TN 20, find the first point where the flow stops. Someone can then open the right panel or follow the damaged line.

## Ninshou
{:#ninshou-actions}

**Read the Technique — Speed 4.** Study visible handseals, a Jutsu in Delay or a maintained effect. Roll Ninshou against TN 10 + 5 per rank (E counts as 1). On success, identify the rank, keywords and action type. The signs reveal what the technique does. Its user keeps any secret Heritage requirements.

**Explain the Counter — Speed 3.** Talk an ally through a Jutsu you identified. Ask the GM one question about its printed rules, such as “Where does its area end?” or “What breaks the effect?” Give the answer to anyone who can hear. Identifying the technique was the check.

## Lore
{:#lore-actions}

**Recall the Hunt — Speed 5.** Examine a monster or evidence of its passage. On Lore TN 20, ask one question its Tracks or Nature Intel can answer, such as what left the marks or why it stopped here. The GM gives one relevant fact. Hunt Intel is earned through the usual hunt actions.

**Read the Crest — Speed 4.** Study a clan mark, maker’s stamp or hunting trophy. On Lore TN 15, the GM identifies its known origin or customary use. If its origin is obscure, learn who or where you could ask about it.

## Espionage
{:#espionage-actions}

**Misdirect — Speed 4.** Give a visible observer a plausible reason to look elsewhere: a dropped parcel, a call from the queue, a familiar name. Beat their Awareness with Espionage to draw their attention long enough for an ally to pass a note or conceal a small object. Crossing a watched space still uses the ordinary Stealth rules.

**Quick Sabotage — Speed 6, Touch.** Wedge an unattended mundane mechanism with something at hand. Roll Espionage against its disarm TN, or TN 20 if none is given. On success it jams until someone reaches it and spends a Speed 4 Utility Action clearing the obstruction. Use a trap’s own rules to disarm it permanently.

## Medicine
{:#medicine-actions}

**Stabilise — Speed 10, Touch.** Spend a First Aid Kit to treat Bleed or a Wound that permits field treatment. Use the [Stabilise action]({{ '/rules/core/#stabilise' | relative_url }}) for the Medicine requirement, TN and treatment effects.

**Field Diagnosis — Speed 4, Touch.** Examine a willing or helpless patient. On Medicine TN 15, identify one current physical status or Wound and learn whether it needs supplies, time or specialist treatment. If the patient has several injuries, you may ask which is most urgent.

## Research
{:#research-actions}

**Find the Pattern — Speed 5.** Compare two observations of the same attack, patrol or routine. On Research TN 20, the GM points out one detail that recurred both times. Use it to pick a vantage point, plan an approach or decide what to watch for next.

**Decode — Speed 6, Touch.** Study a written seal, inscription or control panel. On Research TN 20, or its listed decoding TN, learn one operating instruction, activation condition or warning. Using what you deciphered follows the object’s rules.

## Resistance
{:#resistance-actions}

**Brace Yourself — Speed +2, Defense Link.** When defending against Knockback with a solid surface or fixed handhold within reach, describe how you brace. Before rolling Defense, add 2 Speed to it for Interrupt validity and IC. On Resistance TN 20, reduce the resulting Knockback by up to 3 yards. Resolve damage and other effects normally. Use once per Defense.

**Endure — Speed 5.** Choose a brief task made difficult by ordinary exposure, such as keeping a lantern steady in freezing rain while someone reads a map. On Resistance TN 15, you hold out long enough to finish that task. Resolve any hazard damage and required status checks as usual.

## Stealth
{:#stealth-actions}

**Quiet Advance — Speed 5, Movement.** At Stealth 1, move up to half your ordinary Speed 5 movement distance along continuous cover. Reduce your Stealth TN by 2 for this action instead of the usual 5. Enemies can still Search.

**Lay a False Trail — Speed 5.** Where your passage leaves signs, describe one false clue and the direction it suggests. Roll Stealth once and keep the total. A tracker following those signs who fails to match it with Survival follows the false clue as far as the next fork or clear break in the trail.

## Survival
{:#survival-actions}

**Pick a Route — Speed 4.** Examine a visible stretch of rough ground up to 10 yards long. On Survival TN 15, the GM shows you the safest available footing or tells you where the route becomes unsafe. Anyone following your marked line treats ordinary difficult or Rocky Terrain as one severity lower there, until conditions change.

**Bait — Speed 5.** Place suitable food, scent or a familiar sound within 15 yards of a non-human creature. Beat its Awareness with Survival to draw its attention to that spot. An undisturbed animal approaches if the way looks safe. In a fight it may glance over or give away its position by answering the call. It still chooses its own target.

## Diplomacy
{:#diplomacy-actions}

**Rally — Speed 4.** Give a frightened, willing bystander one clear instruction they can follow: get under the bridge, carry a message, take the child’s hand. On Diplomacy TN 15, they collect themselves and attempt it on their next opportunity if it looks within their ability. An ally played by another player chooses their own response.

**Challenge — Speed 5.** Address someone who can understand you and give them a reason to answer. Beat their choice of Resistance or Espionage with Diplomacy to get a brief hearing for one question or offer. They choose their reply, and anyone in danger can keep acting.

## Artisan
{:#artisan-actions}

**Field Repair — Speed 8, Touch.** With suitable tools and access to a weapon in your discipline, roll Artisan against TN 20 to remove 10 WDP. A weapon benefits once per scene. Damage Levels and broken weapons need ordinary repairs.

**Reinforce — Speed 6, Touch.** Lash a cracked pack frame, splint a tent pole or shore up similar mundane gear in your discipline. With tools and suitable material, Artisan TN 15 keeps it usable for one crossing, load or comparable task. It needs a proper repair afterwards.

## Toxicology
{:#toxicology-actions}

**Identify Toxin — Speed 4.** Examine a sample, secretion or willing patient. Roll Toxicology against TN 10 + Poison severity, or TN 20 for a toxin without severity. On success, learn its listed effects, duration and route of exposure, where known.

**Neutralize Residue — Speed 6, Touch.** Use clean water and one Antidote to clear an unapplied Contact or Injury coating from a willing creature’s equipment or an unattended object. Roll Toxicology against the application TN, or TN 20 if none is listed. Spend the Antidote on the attempt. Poison already in someone’s system needs treatment.
