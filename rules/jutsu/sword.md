# Sword Bukijutsu
{:#sword-bukijutsu}

*Sword techniques exploit an opponent’s habits. Change your angle, catch a familiar defense and finish through the opening.*

## E-Rank
{:#e-rank-10 .jutsu-rank-heading}

#### Kiriage - Rising Cut
{:#rising-cut .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You cut upward beneath the target’s guard.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: +max(2, floor(STR/15))dS  
Speed: \-1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, note the Defense used. If the target uses that broad Defense against your next Sword Chain Attack, it takes a penalty of Athletics/5 and its final Speed is doubled when advancing its next action. This does not affect Interrupt validity.

#### Nukiuchi - Draw Cut
{:#draw-cut .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You strike as the sword leaves its sheath.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+4  
Damage: +max(1, floor(STR/15))dS  
Speed: \+0  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. Reduce this Attack’s final Speed by 2 after modifiers, subject to normal minimums. If the target has not yet attacked you this combat, gain +25% Wounding. The sword ends drawn.

#### Kirioroshi - Descending Cut
{:#descending-cut .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You bring the blade down through the opening made by your previous strike.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: +max(3, floor(STR/15))dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 2, Sword, Attack  
Effects: If your previous Chain attack was Chain 1, choose one when this Attack fully hits: Drop Weight — treat this Attack as 4 Speed higher for damage only; or Smother the Guard — note the Defense used against this Attack; that same broad Defense takes −Athletics/5 against your next Sword Chain attack.

#### Todome Giri - Finishing Cut
{:#finishing-cut .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You commit to a precise cut through the exposed target.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: +max(5, floor(STR/15))dS  
Speed: \+3  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Gain +30% Wounding and reduce Called Shot penalties by Athletics/5, minimum 0. If your previous Sword Chain Attack hit through Partial Defense, use +50% Wounding instead. This exploits a weakened guard without requiring an already-broken Chain.

#### Hanpo - Half-Step
{:#half-step .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You change position while keeping your blade between you and the target.

Rank: E  
Stamina: +4  
Speed: +1  
Tags: Link, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Link to a Sword Attack. If the target Dodges, double that Defense’s final Speed when advancing its next action. This does not change whether the Defense is a valid Interrupt.

#### Iaidō - Way of the Draw
{:#iaido .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">E-Rank</span>

You draw and strike in one motion from a still stance.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Damage: +max(3, floor(STR/15))dS  
Speed: \-2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. On the first use against a creature this combat, its Defensive Interrupts count as \+2 Base Speed for Defense Validity. Ends drawn.

## D-Rank
{:#d-rank-10 .jutsu-rank-heading}

#### Kaeshi Giri - Reversing Cut
{:#reversing-cut .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">D-Rank</span>

You reverse the blade across the line of your previous attack.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: +max(3, floor(STR/10))dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Read the Defense used against your previous Chain Attack. If the target repeats that broad Defense, it takes a penalty of Athletics/5. If it changes Defense, this Attack gains +25% Final Damage.

#### Sakaba - Reverse Edge
{:#reverse-edge .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">D-Rank</span>

You turn the blade’s recovery into a second cut.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+10  
Damage: +max(5, floor(STR/10))dS  
Speed: \-1  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack  
Effects: If the previous Chain Attack was partially defended, impose a penalty of Athletics/5 on the target’s Defense against this Attack.

#### Karame Modoshi - Bind and Recover
{:#bind-and-recover .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">D-Rank</span>

You catch the attacking weapon and guide it aside.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with +Athletics/5. On a full success, your next Sword Chain Attack against that attacker gains +Athletics/5 Accuracy if it is your next non-Interrupt Action.

#### Tanren no Ha - Tempered Edge
{:#tempered-edge .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">D-Rank</span>

Chakra is run along the cutting geometry of the weapon, tightening the edge and keeping it from skidding or twisting on hard contact.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 12  
Upkeep: 2  
Speed: 7  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Utility  
Effects: One sword gains +15% Wounding, stacking with its printed Sword Bukijutsu Wounding. Its edge may cut chakra-created physical bindings as an ordinary weapon Attack against that binding, using the binding’s normal durability and destruction rules. This grants no ability to cut intangible possession or effects without a physical target.

## C-Rank
{:#c-rank-10 .jutsu-rank-heading}

#### Hantō Zuki - Half-Sword Thrust
{:#half-sword-thrust .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">C-Rank</span>

You shorten the sword and drive the point through a seam in the target’s protection.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: +max(5, floor(STR/7))dS  
Speed: \+2  
Accuracy: \+1  
Tags: Chain 1, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Treat this Attack as Piercing. Reduce the target armor’s Absorption and flat DR by 25% for this Attack, after applying its damage-type matchup.

#### Hyōshi Kuzushi - Broken Rhythm
{:#broken-rhythm .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">C-Rank</span>

You feint with your shoulders and blade before striking along another line.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 7  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility  
Effects: Oppose your chained target’s Awareness with Espionage. On success, its next Defense against your Sword Chain Attack takes a penalty equal to 2 plus 1 per full 5 points you won by, maximum 6. The opening ends when used or when the target completes its next non-Interrupt Action.

#### Engetsu Nuki - Circular Draw
{:#circular-draw .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">C-Rank</span>

You sweep the blade from its sheath in a broad arc.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+13  
Damage: +max(6, floor(STR/7))dS  
Speed: \+4  
Line: 3, 1  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Sword, Attack  
Effects: Attack every chosen enemy in the line with one Accuracy roll; divide Speed×PDB evenly among targets hit.

## B-Rank
{:#b-rank-10 .jutsu-rank-heading}

#### Geigeki Nuki - Intercepting Draw
{:#intercepting-draw .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">B-Rank</span>

You draw your sword into the path of an incoming attack.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 12  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Attack, Interrupt  
Effects: Requires a sheathed sword. Parry a Melee Attack with +Athletics/5. On a full success, immediately make a Sword Basic Weapon Attack against the attacker at full damage if it is within Reach. The counter has its own Accuracy and Defense; its action time is included in this technique, and you pay the Basic Attack’s Stamina. End drawn.

#### Nōtō - Draw Reset
{:#draw-reset .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">B-Rank</span>

You sheath the sword quickly, readying another draw.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 8  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility  
Effects: Sheathe one sword you hold without changing Chain value. If your next non-Interrupt Action is a Sword Chain 1, reduce its final Speed by 25%, rounded down, subject to normal Speed minimums, and gain +Athletics/5 Accuracy on that Attack.

## A-Rank
{:#a-rank-10 .jutsu-rank-heading}

#### Shūen no Maai - Final Measure
{:#final-measure .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">A-Rank</span>

You drive a decisive cut through the opponent’s exposed guard.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+24  
Damage: +max(12, floor(STR/3))dS  
Speed: \+4  
Special: Knockback (Carry Through; see Effects)  
Tags: Chain 3, Sword, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Gain +25% Final Damage and +25% Wounding. Choose Sever Guard or Carry Through when declaring. Sever Guard: if a weapon Blocks or Parries this Attack, after defense it suffers WDP equal to its damage-level threshold (Durability + wielder’s DEX/10), even if no damage reaches its wielder. Carry Through: on a full hit resolve Knockback equal to the greater of 10 or STR/3; on a successful Special, you may follow its actual movement, ending within Reach. These are explicit weapon damage and normal resisted Knockback respectively.

#### Mukyoku no Karame - Perfect Bind
{:#perfect-bind .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">A-Rank</span>

You catch the opposing weapon near its base and turn it aside.

Rank: A  
Stamina: 13  
Speed: 3  
Tags: Chain X, Sword, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Parry a Melee Attack with +Athletics/5. On a full success, choose one: Turn the Line — win an Athletics Status Roll against Resistance to move the attacker up to STR/5 yards in any legal direction and apply Prone; or Seal the Line — the attacking weapon cannot Parry your next Sword Chain Attack if it is your next non-Interrupt Action.
