# Club Bukijutsu
{:#club-bukijutsu}

*Club is the force weapon: it wins through impact, resisted Specials, armor pressure, collisions and terrain*.

## E-Rank
{:#e-rank-11}

#### Driving Blow
{:#driving-blow}

You drive a heavy blow through the target.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+3  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+1  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack, Movement  
Effects: On a successful Special, you may follow the target up to half the distance it was actually moved as part of the Attack.

#### Rising Blow
{:#rising-blow}

You swing upward beneath the target’s guard.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+4  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+2  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Stun 3  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack  
Effects: Apply Stun 3 normally. If the target was already Stunned before this Attack hit, do not apply this Stun and add \+2dS damage instead.

#### Returning Swing
{:#returning-swing}

You use the club’s return swing to answer the opponent’s attack.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+5  
Damage: \+3dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: If the target attacked you in the last 20 IC, ignore up to 1 point of Accuracy penalties on this Attack. For each ignored point, gain \+1 Damage Bonus for this Attack.

#### Overhead Blow
{:#overhead-blow}

You bring the weapon down in a committed overhead strike.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+5  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Stun 5  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: Must be a Called Shot to the Head. \+5% Wounding. On a full miss, your next Defense before your next action takes \-1.

#### Brace
{:#brace}

You brace the club against an incoming blow.

Rank: E  
Stamina: 4  
Speed: 5  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: Block or Parry a Melee Attack with \+1. The first time each Round the club would gain WDP from that Block or Parry, reduce the WDP gained by 1, to a minimum of 0\.

#### Wide Swing
{:#wide-swing}

You sweep the weapon across several nearby opponents.

Rank: E  
Stamina: \+6  
Damage: \+2dS  
Speed: \+3  
Area: 2  
Special: Knockback 2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Attack  
Effects: Attack all enemies in reach. Divide Speed×PDB evenly among targets hit. Resolve the Special separately against each fully hit target.

## D-Rank
{:#d-rank-11}

#### Rib Shot
{:#rib-shot}

You strike the target’s ribs to disrupt their breathing and posture.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+8  
Damage: \+4dS  
Speed: \+1  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose one: Smother the Breath — the target takes \-2 Accuracy on its next Attack before 20 IC, increased to \-3 if it was already Stunned when this Attack hit; or Rattle the Frame — apply Stun 4\.

#### Armor Crack
{:#armor-crack}

You direct the weapon’s weight into armour or a barrier.

Rank: D  
Stamina: \+12  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-1  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: Before damaging the target, deal 65% of this Attack's final damage to one Armor piece, Barrier, or Construct directly between you and the target.

#### Body Check
{:#body-check}

You drive your shoulder into the target.

Rank: D  
Stamina: 5  
Speed: 4  
Special: Knockback 3  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility, Movement  
Effects: If adjacent to your chained target, resolve the Knockback Special without dealing damage. On success you may move into the space the target left.

#### Head-Heavy Grip
{:#head-heavy-grip}

Chakra and a shifted grip increase the weapon’s leverage.

Rank: D  
Chakra: 14  
Upkeep: 3  
Speed: 8  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Utility  
Effects: One blunt weapon gains \+1 Reach. Whenever one of its Bukijutsu has a printed Knockback Special, increase that printed Knockback by 1 before rolling it. It may deal Blunt wounds even if another property would change its wound type.

## C-Rank
{:#c-rank-11}

#### Low Sweep
{:#low-sweep}

You drive the club through the target’s base instead of through their centre, forcing them to choose between footing and impact.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+9  
Damage: \+5dS  
Speed: \+3  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Knockback 4 or Prone  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, choose Knockback 4 or Prone, then resolve that Special normally.

#### Short Grip
{:#short-grip}

You adjust your grip to regain control of the heavy weapon.

Rank: C  
Stamina: 6  
Speed: 2  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility  
Effects: Remove up to 3 points of temporary Accuracy penalty affecting your next Club Chain attack, or reduce its Delay by 2 (minimum 0).

#### Wallbreaker
{:#wallbreaker}

You strike a wall or defence with the weapon’s full weight.

Rank: C  
Stamina: \+15  
Damage: \+8dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Tags: Bukijutsu, Club, Attack  
Effects: Against Armor, Barriers and unattended objects, this Attack deals \+45% final damage and cannot have its damage reduced below 50% by Partial Success.

## B-Rank
{:#b-rank-11}

#### Guard Rattle
{:#guard-rattle}

You hit the defending weapon or limb hard enough to shake its hold.

Rank: B  
Stamina: \+15  
Damage: \+8dS  
Speed: \+2  
Tags: Chain 2, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a full hit, if the target used a weapon to Block or Parry, that weapon gains \+3 WDP and takes \-3 Parry for 10 IC. Otherwise, apply Stun 5\.

#### Ground Shock
{:#ground-shock}

You slam the weapon into the ground, sending an impact through nearby footing.

Rank: B  
Stamina: 10  
Speed: 4  
Tags: Chain X, Club, Utility  
Effects: Strike the ground; Area 5\. Create Rocky Terrain 2 for 20 IC. No damage is dealt.

## A-Rank
{:#a-rank-11}

#### Collision Breaker
{:#collision-breaker}

You drive the target through the space behind them, turning walls, trees, wreckage, and other bodies into part of the finishing blow.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+26  
Damage: \+13dS  
Speed: \+6  
Accuracy: \-2  
Special: Knockback 6  
Tags: Chain 3, Club, Attack  
Effects: On a successful Special, if the target collides with solid terrain before travelling the full distance, stop the Knockback and apply Stun 4\. If it collides with another creature, that creature must defend against 50% of this Attack’s final damage; this secondary hit cannot Wound or advance Chain.

#### Juggernaut Rush
{:#juggernaut-rush}

You put the whole body behind the weapon and carry the attack through the space the target thought they controlled.

Rank: A  
Stamina: \+22  
Damage: \+10dS  
Speed: \+4  
Accuracy: \-1  
Special: Knockback 6  
Tags: Chain 1, Club, Attack, Movement  
Effects: Before the Attack, you may use Move with an Attack in a straight line, but calculate the distance using the Attack’s full final Speed instead of half. On a successful Knockback Special, you may move into the target’s former space.
