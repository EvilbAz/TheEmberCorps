# Combo Taijutsu
{:#combo-taijutsu}

### Combo Tags
{:#combo-tags}

**Opener:** Starts a combo on the target when it hits. Includes the Attack tag. Most Openers are in [Unarmed Taijutsu]({{ "/rules/jutsu/unarmed/" | relative_url }}).

**Combo:** Links to a Basic Attack, turning it into a Combo jutsu of the linked technique’s rank. Only one Combo jutsu can modify each attack. Includes the Attack tag; dD uses your unarmed attack’s die size.

**Finisher X:** Ends a combo with a Combo Counter of at least X. Includes the Attack tag.

### Combos
{:#combos}

A combo starts with a **Combo Counter (CC) of 0**, tracked against one target. Use the CC you had when you declared the attack. After a Combo-tagged jutsu hits, increase CC by 1. Multiply the technique’s Cadence (listed as Combo Counter) by your CC and apply it to the attack. For example, +1dX damage and −1 Speed per CC grants +3dX damage and −3 Speed at CC 3, and no benefit at CC 0.

An Opener’s **Momentum** lasts until the combo ends and applies only to Combo-tagged jutsu, not Finishers.

If your CC would go below 0 your combo ends.

### Switching Combos
{:#switching-combos}

During a combo, you may use a new Opener to change its Momentum. The new Opener receives the previous Momentum as though it were a Combo jutsu at CC 1. It does not increase your Combo Counter.

### Breaking a Combo Sequence
{:#breaking-a-combo-sequence}

When a Combo ends, remove its Counter and bonuses. It ends when:

- You Wait or take an action without the Combo tag, except when switching Openers as described above.

- You use any interrupt requiring seals.

- You execute a Finisher.

- You attack a new Target.

- You are Stunned.

Hitting a clone of your intended target does not break your combo, but it does not increase your combo counter.

#### Hangeki Rendan - Counter Combo
{:#hangeki-rendan-counter-combo .jutsu-technique}

<span class="jutsu-rank-badge">C-Rank</span>

You catch a break in your opponent’s rhythm and turn their combo against them.

Stamina: 20  
Speed: 8  
Requires: A Target with an active Combo Counter  
Tags: Utility  
Effects: You and your opponent both make Parry rolls. If your result is higher, you immediately break their Combo and can execute any Opener you know as an Interrupt, with a starting Combo Counter equal to whatever your opponent’s was when you used this technique. If you fail, your opponent may immediately use any Combo Jutsu they know against you as an Interrupt.

Stamina: 10 \+ 5\*X  
Upkeep: 2\*X  
Speed: \+3  
Tags: Utility, Interrupt  
Effects: This is used in response to missing a Finisher with X being your CC prior to using that attack. Your combo is not broken, instead your Combo Counter is reduced by 1\.
