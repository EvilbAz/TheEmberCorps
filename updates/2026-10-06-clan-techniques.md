# Ember Corps — Clan Technique Changelog

**Comparison:** live Ember Corps rules → updated *Clan Techniques — Player Edition*  
**Date:** 6 October 2026  
**Live baseline:** [TheEmberCorps, commit 9be37bf](https://github.com/EvilbAz/TheEmberCorps/commit/9be37bf12aaa93166750d632c9c7c356390a1cc8) · [Clans & Lineages source](https://github.com/EvilbAz/TheEmberCorps/blob/9be37bf12aaa93166750d632c9c7c356390a1cc8/rules/clans.md)

This changelog covers the **43 entries present in the updated Player Edition**, including Water Sky Convergence. It compares against live rules, not earlier draft revisions. Techniques absent from this document are outside its scope; their absence does not remove them from the live game. Crimson Nail includes the restoration requested on 6 October.

**Gameplay changes** below alter costs, effects, restrictions or available options. **Clarifications** specify how the revised rules resolve. Unlisted numerical statistics remain unchanged. Tag and terminology changes are noted where they affect use.

## Yōton

### 1. Caustic Passion

- **Gameplay:** Replaces the separate option to melt up to CHA/10 located projectile weapons with a Defensive Interrupt against an attack made entirely of ordinary metallic projectile weapons. This uses a Ninjutsu Parry at −3 and normal Interrupt legality.
- **Gameplay:** The utility option can melt a selected volume of up to 1 cubic foot from a larger unattended metal or stone structure; live limited the size of the object itself.
- **Clarification:** Range is explicitly CHA/2. A successful Parry negates the attack and melts the weapons; a partial defense reduces the attack before melting them; a full failure leaves them intact. Chakra-reinforced and chakra-created weapons are excluded.

### 2. Lava Release: Gates of Brimstone

- **Gameplay:** Replaces unrestricted DR reduction, wound-based severity bonuses and negative-DR damage amplification with **Searing Residue**. A damaging hit applies the linked rank in Severity, reduced by Partial Defense. Ranks are E=1 through A=5.
- **Gameplay:** Further applications add half the new Severity, rounded up, to a maximum of 8. Residue reduces DR only against the caster’s Yōton attacks and Magma damage, never below 0.
- **Gameplay:** The 20-IC duration refreshes through a new Residue application rather than any Yōton hit. A Speed 5 cleaning Action removes 2 Severity from yourself or a willing creature in Melee reach.
- **Clarification:** Residue starts after the linked hit, applies before fractional DR penetration, and neither damages nor immobilizes by itself. All three expressions can use it; the Ground-Based tag is removed. Link costs remain +4 Chakra plus rank, −2 Accuracy, +2 Speed and +4 Seal Speed.

### 3. Yōton: Yōgan Yoroi — Lava Armour

- **Gameplay:** Replaces flat DR 15 and CHA retaliation with three distinct defensive expressions.
- **Magma:** Halves Katon and Doton damage. Unarmed hits or five IC of bodily contact cause CHA/10 Energy retaliation that ignores DR, once per creature per five IC; it cannot Wound or trigger expression riders.
- **Quicklime:** Halves Katon and Raiton damage. A melee hit triggers opposed Chakra Control versus Resistance; a win adds Quicklime 2, maximum 8. Armour-only Quicklime expires 20 IC after its last application. Ordinary metallic projectiles melt after hitting.
- **Rubber:** DR changes from 25 to CHA/2, or CHA against Blunt damage. A melee attacker in reach can be pushed up to five yards once per five IC.
- **Clarification:** One armour can be active; protection starts after the activation cost, and matching two listed elements does not apply the same percentage reduction twice.

### 4. Lava Release: Nine Hells Erupting Armageddon

- **Gameplay:** Establishes **Range 40** and requires three visible exposed-ground points at least 20 yards apart. Only creatures touching the affected ground are attacked. Adds Ground-Based.
- **Gameplay:** Repeat eruptions use current Ninjutsu Accuracy instead of base Accuracy at Fatigue 0. The separate live **Ignite 12** special is removed; expression effects govern the eruptions.
- **Gameplay:** Adds complete Quicklime and Rubber terrain options. Quicklime adds 1 for entering or travelling three yards, once per five IC across the vents, excluding the caster. Rubber adds up to three yards to forced movement beginning in a vent.
- **Clarification:** Special Upkeep begins on release; the first eruption occurs after Delay 15. Further eruptions occur 15, 30 and 45 IC later. Each vent is defended against separately, including overlaps. Links are paid once and apply to the initial eruptions only.
- **Clarification:** Initial damage remains 10d20!>8; repeats remain 5d20!o>16 with Speed 15. The explosion thresholds and reroll limits are written out. Magma progresses 8 → 6 → 4 → 2, with explicit field duration and overlap ticks. Only one casting may be maintained; ending Upkeep cancels future eruptions, not already-created terrain.

### 5. Yōton: Kazanryū — Volcanic Surge

- **Gameplay:** Formalizes the line as a 6-yard-wide, 20-yard-long strip starting at the caster, replacing the separate Range 20 entry with Range Self. Adds an optional five-yard push on a full hit.
- **Gameplay:** Magma becomes Severity 4 for 20 IC. Quicklime adds 2 when entering or travelling three yards, once per five IC, maximum 8, excluding the caster.
- **Gameplay:** Rubber’s doubled forced movement and five-yard ally bounce become up to five extra yards of forced movement plus 25% higher AGI for the caster and chosen allies moving along the strip.
- **Clarification:** The strip follows connected ground and stops at walls or gaps. The attack resolves before terrain is created. Rubber uses the greater of the technique’s push and its normal Knockback; overlapping Rubber bonuses do not add together. Damage remains 10d12.

### 6. Yōton: Karyū Shōten — Eruption Ascendant

- **Stats:** Speed **26 → 24**; adds **Range 30** and explicit Ground-Based targeting. Damage remains 12d12; Area remains 15.
- **Gameplay:** The 30-IC field now allows later ranged Yōton Ninjutsu to originate from a visible point within it, including attacks against targets outside the field.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces movement-triggered 3d10 Magma with **Magma 6**, increased by 2 Severity for damage against targets bearing your Searing Residue. Replaces periodic Quicklime 2 with +1 to Quicklime applied by your damaging Yōton hits on creatures touching the field.
- **Gameplay:** Rubber adds up to five yards of forced movement and permits redirection up to **180°**, replacing the live 90° redirection.
- **Clarification:** One fixed field per caster; the initial attack occurs before its benefits. Later attacks still need perception, a clear path, normal costs and handseals.

## Jūgo’s Bloodline

### 7. Kassokuken — Piston Fist

- **Gameplay:** Changes from a post-hit addition scaling with Power Attack to a declared Link for an arm-based Basic Unarmed Attack or Unarmed/Combo Taijutsu.
- **Stats:** Replaces variable extra Stamina and dice with **Stamina +5, damage +2d8 and Speed +2**.
- **Gameplay:** Adds Knockback 4, increased to 6 with Jet Booster active on the arm or back.
- **Clarification:** The piston needs no separate Shape Change or body-region allocation and retracts after the attack. Partial Defense reduces Knockback normally.

### 8. Masakari — Weapon Form

- **Stats:** Chakra **10+X/2 → 12**; Upkeep **1+ceil(X/10) → 2**; Speed **10 → 6**; removes Seal Speed 10.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces Artisan-built weapons with one Small or Medium melee weapon arm using an unmodified equipment size/class profile. Removes the two-weapon activation, automatic dual property, crafted upgrades and damage-taken percentage bonus.
- **Gameplay:** Adds **Reshape, fixed Speed 2**, to change the profile or transfer it to the other arm. Handseals require restoring the hand rather than paying +4 Seal Speed per weapon.
- **Clarification:** The weapon is a chakra-reinforced natural limb, uses PDB and qualifies for its weapon class’s Bukijutsu. It has no separate Durability or WDP; attacks against it affect the user. A two-handed profile still requires the other hand, and the limb cannot share a region with Carapace.

### 9. Saibō Kyūin — Cellular Regeneration Absorption

- **Gameplay:** Replaces automatic, progressively cheaper repeat hits with a fresh Submission check and **Stamina 24 / Control 5 on every use**.
- **Gameplay:** Damage now includes PDB × Final Speed. The extra dice are d8s equal to Control remaining after payment, rather than undefined dX dice with DB excluded.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces 1% of damage per IC for ten IC with immediate recovery of **half the Vitality and HP actually removed**, rounded down, capped at RES×2 Vitality and the user’s maximum.
- **Clarification:** Requires a pinned living target and a free hand; living Summons qualify. Breaking contact ends the Submission and adds the normal five IC recovery.

### 10. Saibō Haishutsu — Cellular Regeneration Ejection

- **Stats:** Changes from Taijutsu to Ninjutsu, adds Touch range and removes Delay 10. Chakra 24 and Speed 10 remain.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces the ten-IC transfer of 1% maximum HP per IC with an immediate **1–3 SC** expenditure. Each charge costs ceil(RES/2) HP and restores RES×2 Vitality to another willing living creature.
- **Gameplay:** Spending three SC also suppresses the penalties of one eligible Major or Severe physical Wound for 20 IC. It does not restore missing parts or affect Critical Wounds.
- **Clarification:** The caster must retain at least 1 HP; healing cannot exceed the recipient’s maximum. Reapplication refreshes suppression rather than stacking it.

### 11. Senninka — Sage Transformation

- **Stats:** Removes Delay 10 and the Link tag; makes the technique a self-transformation. Chakra 20, Special Upkeep 10 and Speed 10 remain.
- **Gameplay:** Grants 2 SC on activation once per combat and no longer ends at 0 SC. Retains the current-Willpower cap and faster generation while transformed, with progress carried through form changes.
- **Gameplay:** Raises Natural Mutation to four ranks and lets one Speed 2 Shape Change alter two regions. Defines temporary Mutation Library SC expiry after the second subsequent non-Interrupt Action.
- **Weapon Surge:** Replaces temporary Artisan upgrades with 1–3 SC for +2d8 base damage per SC on a Weapon Form attack.
- **Sage Burst:** Retains rank-based SC cost and halved attack Speed, while explicitly retaining normal Speed floors and Delay 4. Movement uses pre-halving Speed plus 10 + five times rank.
- **Sage Jump:** Defines J SC, Chakra 10+3J and Speed 2+J for J leaps of up to NDB yards. Ground effects apply at landings. Adds an explicit Dodge Link option; removes the discretionary pauses between leaps.
- **Lotus Volley:** Defines L SC and one shot every five IC, with Chakra 10 rolled separately for each. Each shot uses current Ninjutsu Accuracy, Basic Unarmed dice and **NDB×5 instead of the live Speed-3 damage calculation**. Gives exact next-Action timing, interruption rules and cancellation conditions.

## Senju

### 12. Jukai Shinshoku — Sea of Trees Erosion

- **Gameplay:** Replaces moving an existing terrain instance at CHA/80 yards per IC with growing one exposed edge by **1–5 yards** across connected ground.
- **Stats:** Chakra **10+X/3 → 10+2X**; variable channelled Speed becomes **6+X**; adds **Range NDB×5**.
- **Clarification:** New squares must remain in Range. A creature in newly grown squares adds X yards to its thorn tally for Immobilization only, capped at Severity 3 through growth. No immediate thorn damage is added; re-growing an existing square does not repeatedly increase the tally.

### 13. Mokuton: Jukai Kōtan — Nativity of a Sea of Trees

- **Stats:** Chakra **5+X → 10+X**; Speed **8 → 14**; adds explicit **8d10 Blunt damage**. X runs from 5 to floor(CHA/5), with an upper limit of at least 5.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces overwriting all terrain effects with clearing mundane loose cover and Rocky Terrain. Buildings, barriers and maintained Jutsu remain; thorns grow in open space.
- **Gameplay:** Building-mode Chakra changes **5+2X → 10+2X**. Building squares receive CHA HP and defined clearing rules, without thorn damage.
- **Clarification:** Initial defenses resolve before the forest grows. Existing building-quality thresholds remain, including a second storey at Chakra Control 31+. Occupied spaces remain open.

### 14. Mokuton: Mokuryū — Wood Dragon

- **Gameplay:** A full hit establishes a Clinch and Immobilization 4. A partial hit causes reduced Immobilization for ten IC without a lasting dragon.
- **Gameplay:** The dragon becomes an independently attackable creation with **HP CHA×3 and DR floor(CHA/5)**. It cannot defend or Pin; destroying it ends the hold.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces −2 to the next Chakra Exhaustion roll per ten IC with a persistent **−1 per ten IC, maximum −4**, lasting until release. Upkeep now falls by 1 on each actual victim Chakra Exhaustion failure, rather than automatically every ten IC; minimum 2 remains.
- **Clarification:** Gives the dragon separate Control tracking, the victim’s normal escape options, a 50-yard tether, one-dragon limit and explicit release conditions. The caster’s unrelated Actions do not alter its Control.

## Nara

### 15. Kagemane no Jutsu — Shadow Imitation Technique

- **Retained:** Live action mirroring, suspension of the victim’s independent initiative, mirrored movement, copied costs and Accuracy, forced counterattacks, unknown-Jutsu surcharges and the choice to permit or deny a defense. The principal numerical stat block and escape formula remain.
- **Clarification:** X counts prior successful Kagemane castings plus prior Shadow Sewing castings, once per casting. Escape TN is fixed at capture; free checks occur every 15 IC and after each Fatigue category gained. Failed checks grant +2 to later attempts, and denying a defense grants the immediate +5 escape attempt.
- **Added rules:** Defines a continuous shadow path, fixed mirror line, obstacles and a tether ending beyond twice capture Range. Ordinary lighting changes do not automatically break possession.
- **Added rules:** Establishes Control 5 for the shadow and 0 for the victim for linked Grapples. Defines copied handseals, impossible actions, ongoing Upkeep, defensive movement, Stun and prevention of recursive copying.
- **Added rules:** Release returns the victim to initiative at **current IC +1**, plus remaining Stun. A defense taken after escaping adds its normal Interrupt Speed. Timed effects and AP generation continue while possessed.

### 16. Kage Nui no Jutsu — Shadow Sewing Technique

- **Gameplay:** Explicitly applies **Immobilization X**, reduced by Partial Defense; live specified a maintained effect but did not state this restraint Severity.
- **Gameplay:** Adds a Speed 5 escape Action: Resistance versus Chakra Control removes 2 Severity, plus 1 per full five-point margin. The Kagemane Accuracy reduction uses the restraint’s current Severity, not its original X.
- **Clarification:** X is 1–6. Each casting counts toward Kagemane escalation whether it hits or misses. Capture by Kagemane releases the needles; repeated applications on one target replace rather than stack. Multiple Shadow Bind shares one Upkeep X for the casting.

### 17. Kagebuki Shibari — Shadow Neck-Binding Technique

- **Gameplay:** Adds **Takedown** to the eligible Trapping, Ground Fighting and Submission links so the shadow can establish the Pin those techniques require.
- **Clarification:** The linked technique must be known; retain Control and other costs, convert Stamina to Chakra and add Chakra 5 / Speed 2. Use the recorded shadow Control and positional requirements.
- **Added rules:** The victim defends with Grapple Defense against Kagemane’s escape TN. A successful defense avoids this linked effect without ending possession. CHA replaces STR and NDB replaces PDB for its calculations.
- **Clarification:** A pinned victim does not mirror movement. Possession ending releases all shadow holds; Submission recovery and Suffocation fade apply normally.

### 18. Kageyose no Jutsu — Shadow Endgame Technique

- **Retained:** The four options—Retrieve, Grab, Bind and Attack—plus AP-based use every ten IC, NDB damage and +4 Grapple Offense.
- **Gameplay:** Limits sustained restraint to one creature and held weapons to Small or Medium. Weapon Focus does not apply to tendril attacks.
- **Clarification:** Defines when AP options can be declared, effective Speed and rounding, additional AP reductions, normal defender IC costs and the exclusion of Links and attack movement. Recasting does not refresh the timer.
- **Clarification:** Retrieve moves held objects as well as collecting them. Grab establishes a normal Clinch; Bind observes Control and position requirements. Defines weapon origins, Range, WDP, separate hold tracking, shadow obstruction and release conditions. The tendrils have no separate HP.

## Hōzuki

### 19. Suika no Jutsu — Hydration Technique

- **Gameplay:** Suiton damage taken changes **10% → 25%**. The former blanket 10% Taijutsu damage becomes 25% from non-AoE physical Blunt, Slashing or Piercing attacks; other attacks use the stated table.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces broad elemental-strength wording with explicit Raiton ×1, Doton ×1.5 and Katon ×0.5. Mixed elements use the highest applicable multiplier. Raiton Paralysis still ends the form after the hit.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces the endlessly reusable ten gallons costing 5 damage per gallon with the existing **Water Reserve** resource. Invested gallons leave the reserve; one quarter is consumed and the remainder becomes battlefield water.
- **Clarification:** Defines the Speed 4 Interrupt against a non-AoE physical attack, requiring no defensive roll. Voluntary re-forming remains Speed 5. Physical Grapples end, while shadow possession and seals remain effective.
- **Clarification:** Retains reduced movement, slower seals, reduced Taijutsu damage and inaccessible absorbed equipment, with an explicit Crashing Tsunami exception. Poison/Burn suppression, Ignite removal, liquid handseals and dependent-form endings are specified.

### 20. Masakana Namiochiru — Demon Fishwave

- **Stats:** Speed **14 → 12**; explicitly requires Hydration.
- **Gameplay:** Removes the automatic 50% Block. Retains DR equal to half held gallons and +1 PDB/NDB per full 20 gallons, the inability to Dodge, and Doton/Raiton interaction with the DR.
- **Gameplay:** Allows Basic Unarmed Attacks and Crashing Tsunami. Defines absorption from available water within one yard; absorbed equipment’s contents and Water Reserve are separate and unavailable to this absorption.
- **Clarification:** Maintains a CHA-gallon cap and a counted mass pool. Fuel spent on Suiton leaves the pool before bonuses are recalculated; ending the form deposits remaining mass.
- **Clarification:** Suiton-only hits deal no damage but still apply other effects. Only water remaining after normal consumption is absorbed. Multi-target water is divided once among eligible Fishwaves, preventing duplicate gallons. Mixed-element attacks do not feed the form.

### 21. Crashing Tsunami

- **Stats:** Removes Chakra 20; Stamina **20 → 18**; Speed **20 → 14**; base damage **5d10+50 → (CHA/10)d12**. Replaces Area NDB with one Melee target.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces CHA/2 movement with normal Move-with-Attack, already modified by Hydration, **plus CHA/5 yards**. Hydration does not halve this technique’s damage.
- **Clarification:** Retains collection of up to CHA/5 available battlefield gallons within Fishwave’s cap. Collection and PDB recalculation occur before the strike; terrain still applies along the route.

### 22. Gōsuiwan no Jutsu — Great Water Arm

- **Gameplay:** Replaces unrestricted one-handed use of any weapon with one-handed use of a two-handed weapon while retaining normal size and proficiency requirements.
- **Clarification:** X is an integer from 1 to floor(CHA/30), with an upper limit of at least 1. Benefits apply to attacks using the enlarged arm; doubled PDB applies against unattended objects, barriers and structures, not creatures, Clones, Puppets or worn equipment.
- **Clarification:** One arm effect at a time. Full Hydration suppresses its benefits while Upkeep continues; Partial Hydrification does not. Defines Knockback rounding, handseal use and what happens to a two-handed weapon when the arm returns to normal.

### 23. Obomizu Katamari — Drowning Water Blob Technique

- **Gameplay:** Uses **Ninjutsu Accuracy instead of Taijutsu Accuracy**. Replaces the Arm Bar/Knee Bar simulation with **Immobilization 6**, reduced by Partial Defense, while the victim retains its Actions.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces Break Grapple with a Speed 6 Athletics-or-Resistance check against Chakra Control. A win removes 3 Severity, a win by ten removes all, and each failed attempt adds +2 to later attempts.
- **Retained:** Suffocation increases by 1 every ten IC and moving beyond three yards ends the hold.
- **Clarification:** One Blob per caster; its water is part of the caster’s body and can be attacked at the victim’s position. An Area covering both locations hits the caster only once. Defines Upkeep, Hydration and zero-Severity endings.

### 24. Suika Bunretsu — Liquefaction Split

- **Stats:** Speed **12 → 8**; gallon expenditure becomes **0–5** rather than the live 0–10 notation.
- **Gameplay:** Parry bonus changes **+G → +floor(G/2)**. Replaces the split-body/attack-move description with movement of **max(2, 2G) yards** along a continuous route through openings at least one inch wide.
- **Gameplay:** Removes one external physical Immobilization; it does not remove internal conditions, seals or shadow possession. Excludes AoE and Genjutsu attacks as well as the retained Doton/Raiton exclusions.
- **Clarification:** Gallons are consumed from Water Reserve. Success permits escape and movement; partial defense applies the hit first and then movement if still able; full failure leaves the user in place. Ending Hydration during the hit cancels movement.

## Akimichi

### 25. Nikudan Sensha — Human Bullet Tank

- **Retained:** Continuous blocking preparation, Dodge access, no Parry or handseals, −5 Awareness, +10 escape checks and the Immobilization-10 termination threshold.
- **Gameplay:** Limits Immobilization suppression to external physical effects. Explicitly drops held items and releases Grapples the caster controls on entry.
- **Clarification:** Defines the available Action list and the choice between Speed 0 Block and another defense. Suppressed effects retain their durations and return when Rolling ends.
- **Clarification:** Ending Upkeep or losing the size form ends Rolling. Replacing Baika with Chō Baika in the same Action can preserve it.

### 26. Rolling Crush

- **Gameplay:** The five-yard charge doubles **base PDB**, rather than the whole Damage Bonus; temporary DB bonuses are added afterward. Mass Is Momentum still applies normally.
- **Clarification:** Defines movement as AGI×Final Speed/50 with current modifiers, a straight approach, one impact and an Area supplied by the active size technique. The caster is excluded; allies are not.
- **Retained:** Stamina 15, Speed 15, (STR/15)d8 and Parry −3/−5. Both ordinary and spiked Rolling qualify. A miss leaves Rolling active but grants no second charge.

### 27. Nikudan Hari Sensha — Spiked Human Bullet Tank

- **Gameplay:** Explicitly permits Rolling Crush to deal Blunt or Piercing. It remains Unarmed Taijutsu for the attacker’s bonuses but counts as a Weapon Attack for the defender’s Block and Parry.
- **Clarification:** Retains the 30 reusable prepared kunai, Upkeep 4 and 50% blocking of weapon damage. Retaliation uses the DR value applied to that incoming hit before penetration; it cannot Wound or trigger more retaliation.
- **Clarification:** Replaces ordinary Rolling without interrupting it, releases the committed kunai when it ends and permits turns on later Move Actions while each charge remains straight.

### 28. Chōdan Bakugeki — Butterfly Bullet Bombing

- **Gameplay:** Replaces daily Calories and the −5 allowance with **15–30 CP**, or **10–30 in Butterfly Mode**. Missing CP can be funded through Calorie Conversion at 4 HP each while retaining at least 1 HP.
- **Gameplay:** CP used for this attack cannot simultaneously pay its Chakra reduction or Butterfly Mode damage bonus; those expenditures are separate. Normal CP recovery applies.
- **Clarification:** Preserves combined PDB+NDB, full primary-target damage and half secondary-target damage. The caster is excluded from the Area; allies defend normally. Baika and Chō Baika must be inactive at declaration.
- **Clarification:** Chakra Exhaustion uses the cost after Calorie Conversion. A cost reduced to zero needs no exhaustion roll. The original damage, Accuracy, Speed and Area formulas remain.

## Aburame

### 29. Hijutsu: Konchū no Bōdō — Insect Insurrection

- **Gameplay:** Insect removal changes from Speed 5 plus one per additional insect to **Speed 5 to remove up to two discovered female insects**.
- **Clarification:** The five-IC Delay affects placement only. The parent attack resolves normally; each target receives one insect even from a multi-hit attack. Placement follows a moving target.
- **Clarification:** Retains +2 Speed, or +5 for an Area attack, and Stealth TN 25+Stealth bonus. Defines Predatory Cycle and the further five-IC Proliferation placement without recursively triggering it.

### 30. Hijutsu: Kikaichū Mushikui — Destruction Bug Bite

- **Gameplay:** Removes the redundant +X/4 Accuracy entry. The Chakra-modifier roll is now explicitly the only defense, with no attack roll or conventional defense added.
- **Gameplay:** The next-exhaustion penalty now expires after **ten IC**, rather than at the end of the target’s next Action, and shares a −3 cap with Feeding.
- **Clarification:** Requires a target inside the Swarm with at least one female insect. The defensive roll uses current Chakra Exhaustion modifiers but neither spends Chakra nor causes Fatigue.
- **Retained:** 10+3X HP loss, capped at 30 before the 50% failure increase; the TN formula and insect Stealth Level increase remain. Specifies no Wounds, no renewed concealment for discovered insects and normal Living Colony recovery.

### 31. Hijutsu: Mushidama — Bug Sphere

- **Gameplay:** Allows **Insect Screen** and Return while enclosed, in addition to Bug Bite against the enclosed target. Removes the Piercing tag and treats it as Control.
- **Clarification:** Partial Defense reduces Immobilization 2. Dodge uses the original Swarm radius; after capture only the enclosed target counts as inside.
- **Added rules:** Defines how the Swarm follows instantaneous movement, how teleports or excessive distance break the enclosure, and how Area attacks damage a Swarm occupying the target’s space.
- **Clarification:** Every ten IC adds a female insect and resolves Predatory Cycle. Ending Upkeep restores the old radius at the current position; Return uses its own costs and Range. Only one target can be enclosed.

## Yuki

### 32. Suitenhoufukyuu — Water Sky Convergence

- **Gameplay:** Limits water access: ordinary containers, scattered pools and chakra-infused water remain eligible, but water inside a creature or committed to a maintained Jutsu cannot be seized until released. Live expressly allowed taking water from others’ techniques.
- **Gameplay:** Removes the separate freeze/liquefy Action printed here and directs those operations to **Living Ice**, using that feature’s costs, Range and quantity.
- **Clarification:** Preserves Suiton-only handseal substitution, includes a Link’s Seal Speed in the calculation and floors the added Speed at zero. Other Seal Speed reductions do not apply; Hyōton still needs handseals. Water consumption remains tracked.

### 33. Hyōton: Jisarenhyō — Earthen Consecutive Chains of Ice

- **Stats:** Replaces Melee with **Range 15** and explicit Control targeting.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces percentage-of-current-Vitality/HP damage on any Chakra roll with flat Vitality loss of **casting NDB × current temporary Hyōton Immobilization**, capped at NDB×5, once per ten IC. It cannot spill into HP or Wound.
- **Gameplay:** Triggers only after a positive Chakra-costing Action, not passive Upkeep or a roll merely using a Chakra modifier. Removes death after 100 IC for chakra-less victims, the day-long maintenance provision and covert application without Immobilization.
- **Clarification:** Retains initial Immobilization 5 and its ten-IC fade. Temporary ice uses one highest Severity per caster; paid Chakra Actions refresh the timer. Zero Severity, ending Upkeep or exceeding twice Range ends the reactive effect. Permanent Wound freezes cannot sustain it; recasting preserves the reaction timer.

### 34. Hyōton: Sensatsu Kōri — Thousand Ice Needles

- **Gameplay:** Immobilization remains 2 without Ice but becomes **4 when at least one gallon is used**. The +2 Accuracy bonus now requires at least Severity 3 of your Hyōton Immobilization at declaration.
- **Gameplay:** Both created and invested Ice consume only one quarter of G, rounded up, depositing the remainder, rather than describing invested Ice as wholly consumed.
- **Clarification:** Retains 0–10 gallons and 8d10+Gd10. This is one Ninjutsu Area Attack, not a Multi-Throw; its rider replaces the ordinary Hyōton rider and uses the highest temporary Severity rather than stacking.

## Kazekage Clan / Jiton

### 35. Suna no Yoroi — Sand Armor

- **Stats:** Chakra **10+X/2 → 18**; Upkeep **1+ceil(X/10) → 4**; Speed **10 → 8**; removes Seal Speed 10; requires **8 gallons of Sand**.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces Artisan-built armour with a maintained layer granting **DR 12**, or **18 against Piercing or Projectile Weapon attacks**. Removes tripled Absorption and one-third Durability.
- **Clarification:** The layer works alongside worn armour, has no separate HP and cannot be attacked independently. Its Sand remains committed, travels with the caster and is released when it ends; dropping below eight committed gallons ends it. One layer at a time.

### 36. Mujona Kaihō — Merciless World Order

- **Stats:** Chakra **10 → 20**; activation Speed **4 → 8**; Regather **Speed 5 → 4**. Upkeep 4 and Area CHA/2 remain.
- **Gameplay:** Weapon capacity changes **CHA×2 → CHA/2** and is limited to unattended Small metallic projectile weapons. They orbit within one yard of the caster and travel with them.
- **Gameplay:** Replaces any number of AP attacks every ten IC with **one Multi-Throw volley per ten IC**. At least two weapons are required, using the normal weapon limit unless Electromagnetic Dominance is paid for separately.
- **Clarification:** Defines AP declaration windows, half-effective-Speed AP cost rounded up, normal Multi-Throw +0.25 DB per weapon, NDB damage and Stamina converted into an actual Chakra Exhaustion cost.
- **Retained:** CHA-based Accuracy at −3, doubled polarity Accuracy, no polarity Speed benefit, no weapon-specific abilities, charge lost on use and defenses that do not advance IC.
- **Clarification:** Charge changes occur once per volley; effective Speed determines damage and defense legality. Recasting does not refresh the timer, and unused weapons drop when Upkeep ends.

## Uzumaki

### 37. Kingusari no Jutsu — Golden Chain Technique

- **Stats:** Removes **Stamina equal to weapon cost +5**, weapon Delay and weapon Accuracy. Retains Chakra +5 and adds **Speed +2**.
- **Gameplay:** Restores the linked Mark’s normal handseals rather than converting every handseal IC into Delay. Limits the Link to a single-target Mark.
- **Clarification:** The Mark uses its own Accuracy and defense, with Range equal to Chain Range +5 and Projectile replacing Melee. A hit applies the seal without another weapon strike. The Chain must be available; Chakra applies to its WDP once.

### 38. Kusari Gitai — Chain Mimicry
