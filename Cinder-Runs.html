FIELD NOTES / 01

The Cinder Run

Red ribbons stretch between the rooftops of Haven Ember. Lanterns mark the crossings, neighbours lean out of their windows, and someone has already started arguing about the shortest way through the market. When the bell rings, the street empties upwards.

Hunters carry an Ember Seal through rooftops, workshops, gardens and waterways. Couriers race to prove their worth; crews settle rivalries; spectators gather wherever a landing looks particularly ambitious. A careful line can beat a hurried leap, and the finish rarely belongs to whoever boasts loudest at the start.

A Cinder Run is a player-run race for 2–6 Hunters. Each round covers one course section. Gain Progress through your Pace, route and Racing Action. The active racer with the most Progress after the final round wins; tied totals share the placing.

Starting a Race

Choose a Quick Run of 4 rounds, Standard Run of 6, or Endurance Run of 8. The default is a six-round Lantern Circuit with random sections. All racers complete every round. A large Progress total never skips a checkpoint.

Record your Pace, Race Ratings, resource Ratings, starting Fatigue and accumulated resource penalties. Calculate starting Progress using Starting Marks. Begin with 0 Sparks and one use of Burn for the Line. Post these values before the first section is revealed.

A race is one scene. Begin with your normal scene allowance of Willpower, subject to effects that alter recovery. Carry existing Fatigue and injuries into the race. There is no automatic recovery between rounds.

Pace

Determine effective AGI for an ordinary Move, including Runner, equipment and existing movement penalties. Do not count an activated transformation or temporary movement buff.

Starting Marks

Your Seed is your Pace plus the average of your three highest skill Race Ratings, rounded down. Use the seven skills listed under Race Ratings, before Fatigue. Do not add Specialization, Willpower, section bonuses or Racing Action bonuses.

Find the highest Seed among the entrants. Your starting Progress is (highest Seed − your Seed) × number of rounds. These starting marks are the standard handicap for mixed-level races. Everyone still visits the same checkpoints.

Use your actual recorded abilities when calculating Seed; you cannot hold back a bonus to gain a larger starting mark. A deliberate withdrawal does not recalculate anyone’s starting Progress.

FIELD NOTES / 02

Race Ratings

Race Ratings are used for skill, Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls during the race. They do not change your character sheet or any roll outside this activity.

Calculate a Rating

Take the square root of the relevant normal modifier and round down. For a negative modifier, take the square root of its absolute value, round down and restore the minus sign. A modifier of 0 gives a Rating of 0.

Continue the same square-root calculation above the table. There is no upper Rating cap. For example, +7 becomes +2, +30 becomes +5, +60 becomes +7, and −9 becomes −3.

Skill Ratings

Record Athletics, Awareness, Survival, Research, Espionage, Chakra Control and Resistance. Include actual ranks, Skill Focus and unconditional modifiers from abilities, equipment and Uniques before converting. Existing non-Fatigue penalties that apply to that skill are included too.

Apply Fatigue after conversion. Section and Racing Action bonuses are also added afterward. A normal Athletics modifier of +30 gives Rating +5. At Fatigue 2, with Steady Run, the Course Check is 1d20 + 5 − 2 + 2.

When a section lists two skills, choose one in your declaration. Use its Rating and its Specialization, if any. Skill substitution is available only through the Applied Knowledge rule in this activity.

Resource Ratings

Convert your normal Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion modifiers separately. Include permanent bonuses such as Refined Chakra Control, but exclude the +5 per Fatigue level and accumulated resource penalties before converting.

Stamina roll = 1d20 + Stamina Rating + 5 × Fatigue − accumulated Stamina penalty. Chakra Exhaustion uses the same formula with Chakra Rating and its own penalty. Use the adjusted action cost as the TN.

For Shunshin, include any bonus that specifically applies to its Chakra Exhaustion roll before taking the square root; record a separate Shunshin Rating if necessary. Do not add that bonus again afterward.

Using the Character Sheet

Use the modifier your sheet normally applies, not the underlying attribute. A sheet modifier of +16 becomes Rating +4; an attribute of 160 is not substituted for it. The race uses these Ratings instead of the normal full roll modifiers.

FIELD NOTES / 03

Race Rounds

Resolution Order

Before round one, each racer rolls 1d20. Highest resolves first; reroll ties. Keep this order for the whole race. All choices and rolls are public.

Round Sequence

1. Reveal the section. Roll 1d20 on the Course Table. If it matches the previous section, use the next numbered section instead, wrapping 20 to 1. Fixed courses use their listed sequence.

2. Gain Sparks. After round one, each active racer at least 8 Progress behind the leader gains 1 Spark, to a maximum of 2 held. Use current totals, including Starting Marks.

3. Declare. Each racer posts one route, one Racing Action, a Course Skill and any target. Declare in reverse resolution order, so the order of choice is fixed. Choices are final once posted. No checks are rolled until everyone has declared.

4. Resolve actions. In resolution order, make resource rolls or apply Catch Your Breath. Apply Fatigue immediately. Next resolve Cut Across contests in that order. Then resolve Course Checks in that order, except that a Follow the Footsteps check waits for its target. Settle all rerolls and Spark spending for a check before moving on.

5. Advance. Apply everyone’s Progress simultaneously. Then grant any end-of-round Spark from Training Yard. Record resources and begin the next round. After the last round, determine the placings.

A Course Check is 1d20 + the chosen skill’s Race Rating − Fatigue + applicable section and action bonuses. Meeting the TN succeeds. Natural 1 and 20 have no additional effect. A Safe route does not count as a successful Course Check.

Progress gained = Pace + route, action, section and Spark adjustments − any Cut Across penalty. The minimum gain is 0. Catch Your Breath replaces Pace with half Pace, rounded down. Previous Progress is never lost.

Sparks

Hold up to 2 Sparks and spend up to 1 each round. Second Chance: immediately after a Course Check, spend 1 to reroll its d20 and keep the higher die. Burst: after settling your Course Check, spend 1 for +3 Progress. A Safe-route racer may spend Burst when their Course Check would resolve.

A roll may be rerolled once, by either a Spark or Willpower. Sparks cannot reroll resource or Cut Across rolls. Sparks expire when the race ends. Lantern Square changes Burst to +4 Progress.

FIELD NOTES / 04

Racing Actions

Choose one Racing Action each round. Apply its Progress adjustment in addition to Pace and the route result.

Steady Run

You keep your footing and let the course come to you. There will be another place to pass.

Progress: +0 Effect: Gain +2 to your Course Check. On Windbridge, gain +4 instead.

Push the Pace

You lengthen your stride and commit to the next landing, trusting your legs to carry you through.

Stamina: 10 Progress: +4

Make a Stamina roll using your Stamina Rating and apply exhaustion normally. A failed roll still grants the action’s Progress unless you become incapacitated. In Foundry Walk, the cost is 12. On Open Street, a successful resource roll grants another +2 Progress.

Read the Route

A leaning beam, a loose tile, footprints ending at a broken railing. A moment spent looking can save a much longer climb back up.

Progress: −2 Effect: Gain +5 to your Course Check. Requires the Main route or Shortcut.

Cinder Yard, Scrap Maze and Steamworks Passage increase the bonus to +6; Archive Stairs increases it to +7. In Signal Garden, the action has no Progress penalty.

Follow the Footsteps

You watch where the runner ahead lands and follow the line that held their weight.

Progress: +0 Target: One racer strictly ahead of you at the start of the round.

If the target selected the same non-Safe route and succeeds on their Course Check, gain +3 to yours. Otherwise gain no bonus. In Mirror Arcade, the bonus is +4. If several racers follow one another, resolve their checks from the foremost racer backwards. A tied racer is not ahead.

Cut Across

You claim the inside line and make a rival check their stride.

Progress: −2 Target: One other racer within 5 Progress of you at the start of the round.

If you selected the same route, roll opposed Athletics Race Ratings, including Fatigue and Athletics Specialization. Section and other action bonuses do not apply. Win to impose −4 Progress on the target this round; ties favour the target. Willpower is allowed.

A racer can suffer only one −4 Cut Across penalty per round. Later attempts against an already-delayed racer, invalid targets and mismatched routes have no contest and still cost −2. In Bell Alley a successful attempt costs 0. Freight Frames extends the targeting gap to 10. Cut Across cannot be selected on Windbridge, or target a Safe-route racer in Scrap Maze.

FIELD NOTES / 05

Exertion and Recovery

Catch Your Breath

You ease your stride, settle your breathing and let the next stretch take less out of you.

Progress: Half Pace, rounded down Effect: Reduce your accumulated Stamina penalty by 1, plus normal improvements that specifically affect Rest, to a minimum of 0.

Resolve your chosen route normally. This action does not remove Fatigue or reduce Chakra Exhaustion penalties. In Canal Teeth, gain +2 additional Progress.

Shunshin

A burst of chakra carries you past the next ribbon before it finishes turning in the wind.

Requires: Shunshin no Jutsu Chakra: 10 Progress: +4

Make a Chakra Exhaustion roll using your Shunshin Rating. Apply any cost reduction that normally affects this jutsu, respecting its minimum cost. In Steamworks Passage, its base cost is 12 before reductions. In Sluice Steps, gain +1 additional Progress if you pass your Course Check.

This Racing Action replaces Shunshin’s normal movement and AP expenditure. Runner does not increase its Progress. You still resolve your route; the technique grants no automatic course success.

Burn for the Line

The last lanterns are ahead. You push through the ache in your legs and give the course everything left in them.

Stamina: 15 Progress: +7 Limit: Once per race, during either of the final two rounds.

Make a Stamina roll using your Stamina Rating. The use is spent when declared. A failed roll still grants Progress unless you become incapacitated. On Open Street, a successful roll grants another +2 Progress.

Resource Costs

Push the Pace and Burn for the Line are Racing Actions, not Taijutsu or attacks. A reduction restricted to those categories does not apply. A modifier that explicitly affects all Stamina costs still applies. Use the final adjusted cost for both the resource TN and its accumulated penalty.

Fatigue

A successful resource roll adds final cost ÷ 5, rounded down, to that resource’s accumulated penalty. A failed roll raises Fatigue by 1, plus another level for every full 15 points of failure, and clears both accumulated resource penalties. Do not also carry the failed action’s penalty past that reset.

Each Fatigue level gives −1 to racing skill rolls and +5 to resource rolls. Cut Across uses Athletics on both sides, so both apply the skill penalty. These changes apply before subsequent rolls in the round.

A racer who would advance beyond Fatigue 5 withdraws immediately, gains no Progress that round and cannot place. Starting Marks, checkpoints and an asynchronous posting delay do not provide recovery. When the race scene ends, use normal recovery rules.

FIELD NOTES / 06

Character Abilities

Skill Focus and Permanent Bonuses

Include Skill Focus ranks and unconditional skill modifiers when calculating each Race Rating. Bonuses from Basic Training, Reflexes, General Knowledge, Applied Knowledge and similar abilities are converted once as part of the total; they are not added again after conversion.

Conditional bonuses, environmental immunities and special activations grant no additional racing effects unless listed here or in the section. Equipment and existing injuries remain included in the starting modifiers. Racing itself inflicts no Wounds or damage.

Specialization

If you have Specialization in the skill being rolled, treat a d20 result below 8 as an 8. This applies to Course Checks and Athletics contests using that skill. It changes the die, not your Race Rating or Seed. Apply it to a Spark reroll as well.

Applied Knowledge

Its passive rank and skill bonuses contribute to your normal modifiers before conversion. When choosing Read the Route, you may declare Research as your Course Skill on any section. Use your Research Race Rating and Research Specialization, if owned. Keep Read the Route’s normal bonus and Progress adjustment, including section exceptions.

This substitution is part of Read the Route and cannot be used with another Racing Action or on a Cut Across contest. No extra IC is paid during the race.

Willpower

After a Course Check or Cut Across roll, you may spend 1 Willpower to reroll that d20. If the new die is below 11, add 10 to it. Replace the original die with the adjusted result and reapply the same modifiers. You must use the new result.

A roll receives only one reroll, from Willpower or a Spark. Willpower cannot reroll Stamina, Chakra Exhaustion, course generation or setup rolls. In a Cut Across contest, the acting racer decides first whether to use Willpower, then the target; settle both choices before applying the outcome.

Movement and Chakra Training

Runner is included in effective movement AGI for Pace. Wall Walking and Water Walking grant the bonuses listed by each section without a separate roll or cost. Good Lungs improves Catch Your Breath. Refined Chakra Control is included in your normal Chakra Exhaustion modifier before conversion.

Normal AP, action Speed, handseal timing and combat movement are replaced by race rounds. Other jutsu, transformations, clones, companions and summons provide no additional actions, movement or automatic checkpoints. An Ember Seal must remain with its entrant.

Participation

Choose live play or a 24-hour declaration window before starting. A missed declaration becomes Safe route and Steady Run. Two consecutive missed declarations cause withdrawal. After a deadline, another player may publicly roll outstanding dice for a posted declaration, with optional spending only if predeclared.

Correct errors before revealing the next section. Once that section is revealed, completed-round results stand; apply the correct rule going forward. A disputed unlisted interaction grants no bonus. The race has no automatic XP, currency or Infamy award.

FIELD NOTES / 08

Rooftops and Waterways

1 Market Eaves

Laundry runs between the buildings, and the market awnings have been pulled tight. A red ribbon crosses a swinging sign above a stall whose owner is already watching the race with folded arms.

Course Skill: Athletics

Routes: Covered arcade; roof ridges; hanging signboards.

Effect: Wall Walking grants +2 to your Course Check.

2 Cinder Yard

Ash fills the cracks between old paving stones. Stacks of kiln brick divide the yard into narrow passages, and footprints ahead disappear into a heap that has only just stopped moving.

Course Skill: Survival

Routes: Cleared perimeter; rubble lanes; kiln tops.

Effect: Read the Route grants +6 to your Course Check instead of +5.

3 Watchtower Spiral

The checkpoint lantern hangs above a spiral of stairs and repairs. Loose boards knock against the outer scaffolding. From the ground, spectators can see every place a runner nearly misses a foothold.

Course Skill: Athletics

Routes: Inner stairs; scaffold ramps; outer ledges.

Effect: Wall Walking grants +2 to your Course Check. A successful Shortcut grants +1 additional Progress.

4 Canal Teeth

Water rushes through the lock gates. Pontoons bump beneath the footbridge, while the shortest ribbon line stretches across the spillway. The towpath offers an unusually pleasant place to catch your breath.

Course Skill: Athletics

Routes: Towpath; pontoons; spillway.

Effect: Water Walking grants +2 to your Course Check. Catch Your Breath grants +2 additional Progress.

FIELD NOTES / 09

Streets and Roots

5 Signal Garden

Old festival streamers crowd the trees around the checkpoint. Fresh race markers hang among them, but several arrows point towards gates that have long since been bricked shut. The right sequence cuts straight across the garden.

Course Skill: Awareness or Research

Routes: Marked perimeter; lantern trail; signal sequence.

Effect: Read the Route has no Progress penalty.

6 Open Street

For one glorious stretch, the road is straight. Dust rises behind the field as boots strike the old paving. Everyone who has been promising that they were saving their strength suddenly has an audience.

Course Skill: Athletics

Routes: Outer pavement; carriageway; central barriers.

Effect: A successful resource roll for Push the Pace or Burn for the Line grants +2 additional Progress.

7 Bell Alley

Small bells hang from wires between the houses. A patient runner can slip between them. Most choose to announce their arrival to the whole neighbourhood, usually accompanied by language the residents pretend not to hear.

Course Skill: Awareness or Espionage

Routes: Doorstep path; wire gaps; low crossing wires.

Effect: A failed Shortcut gives −5 Progress instead of −7. A successful Cut Across has no Progress cost.

8 Bloomroot Run

Roots have lifted the street into ridges and swallowed the wheels of an abandoned wagon. The direct path passes underneath. It is short, dark and exactly the right height to make someone regret their choice of coat.

Course Skill: Survival or Athletics

Routes: Cleared verge; exposed roots; root tunnel.

Effect: The Safe route has no Progress penalty.

FIELD NOTES / 10

Works and Crossings

9 Sluice Steps

A line of narrow ledges descends beside a sluice. Spray coats the stone and makes each landing shine. The old maintenance stairs are perfectly serviceable, provided nobody minds watching the braver runners pass overhead.

Course Skill: Chakra Control or Athletics

Routes: Maintenance stairs; wet ledges; sluice lip.

Effect: Water Walking grants +2 to your Course Check. Shunshin grants +1 additional Progress if you also pass your Course Check.

10 Scrap Maze

Old carts, hull plates and dismantled engines have been stacked around the salvage yard. Ribbons thread through gaps that look alike until someone notices a hinge, a latch or a fresh scrape on the metal.

Course Skill: Espionage or Awareness

Routes: Marked aisle; stack passages; service hatches.

Effect: Read the Route grants +6 to your Course Check instead of +5. Safe-route racers cannot be targeted by Cut Across.

11 Foundry Walk

Heat rolls from the working furnaces. The course follows a fenced walkway above the loading floor, with a shorter crossing past the vents. The workers below do not interrupt their shift, though several have picked a favourite.

Course Skill: Resistance or Survival

Routes: Outer gantry; furnace walkway; vent crossing.

Effect: Push the Pace costs Stamina 12 instead of 10. Burn for the Line is unchanged.

12 Windbridge

The bridge flexes between two old structures. Ribbons pull sideways in the wind and footsteps travel through the planks ahead of each runner. The handrail is covered in the grip marks of people who discovered confidence was not enough.

Course Skill: Athletics or Chakra Control

Routes: Braced walkway; centre planks; suspension beams.

Effect: Steady Run grants +4 to your Course Check instead of +2. Cut Across cannot be chosen here.

FIELD NOTES / 11

Reflections and Machinery

13 Mirror Arcade

Polished metal and cracked windows catch the checkpoint lantern from several directions. Some runners chase the brightest reflection. Others watch where the people ahead actually turn.

Course Skill: Awareness or Research

Routes: Painted floor arrows; shopfront passage; reflected lantern line.

Effect: Follow the Footsteps grants +4 to your Course Check instead of +3 when its target qualifies.

14 Mosswater Cut

A shallow runoff channel cuts behind the gardens. Moss softens the stone until the first hurried foot lands on it. The red ribbons alternate between the bank and the water, following a path that rewards an even stride.

Course Skill: Survival or Chakra Control

Routes: Gravel bank; stepping stones; mossy channel edge.

Effect: Water Walking grants +2 to your Course Check. Passing the Main route with Steady Run grants +2 additional Progress.

15 Freight Frames

Empty cargo frames stand in rows around the old transport yard. Every gap is wide enough to promise a pass and narrow enough for someone else to spoil it. The checkpoint crew has put the ribbons on the upper rails for a reason.

Course Skill: Athletics or Espionage

Routes: Loading lane; frame interiors; upper rails.

Effect: Cut Across can target a racer within 10 Progress instead of 5. All other targeting restrictions apply.

16 Steamworks Passage

Warm mist drifts between the pipe runs. The lantern appears and vanishes as a valve opens somewhere behind the wall. Footsteps ring on the grating, giving away the turns before the runners come into view.

Course Skill: Resistance or Awareness

Routes: Outer passage; pipe gallery; valve-side grating.

Effect: Read the Route grants +6 to your Course Check instead of +5. Shunshin costs Chakra 12 instead of 10 before reductions.

FIELD NOTES / 12

Gardens and Lanterns

17 Hanging Gardens

Planters and rope walks fill the space between neighbouring roofs. Below them, vines cover a broad maintenance net. A bad landing is usually followed by a long climb and the discovery that the flowers have excellent roots.

Course Skill: Survival or Athletics

Routes: Garden walk; rope bridges; hanging planters.

Effect: Main-route failure gives −2 Progress instead of −4. Shortcut failure gives −5 instead of −7.

18 Archive Stairs

Old district numbers cover the stairwell. Repairs have joined some landings and sealed others, leaving a route that makes more sense on the faded wall plan than it does underfoot. The fastest line starts by reading the signs.

Course Skill: Research or Awareness

Routes: Ribboned stairs; numbered landings; old service route.

Effect: Read the Route grants +7 to your Course Check instead of +5.

19 Training Yard

The Corps has left its balance beams and practice hurdles in place. Familiar drills suddenly look less familiar with a rival taking the next lane. Someone on the fence is counting clean landings loudly enough to annoy everyone.

Course Skill: Athletics or Chakra Control

Routes: Outer track; low hurdles; balance beams.

Effect: Passing the Main route with Steady Run grants 1 Spark after Progress is updated, up to the normal holding limit. It cannot be spent this round.

20 Lantern Square

The course spills into a square full of voices. Red lanterns hang above the barriers, and every racer can hear somebody urging them on. The inside line passes close enough to the crowd to make the next burst feel personal.

Course Skill: Awareness or Athletics

Routes: Outer paving; barrier gaps; inner ribbon line.

Effect: A Spark spent on Burst grants +4 Progress instead of +3.

Ember Relay

Two or three equal-sized teams of two or three Hunters may race over six rounds. Split the rounds into equal consecutive legs and declare the running order before a random course is revealed. Each entrant runs one leg for one team.

Calculate each runner’s Seed normally. A team’s Seed is the average of its runners’ Seeds, rounded down. Compare team Seeds to calculate Starting Marks for six rounds. Progress and Sparks belong to the team; other resources and Fatigue belong to individual runners.

Only the active runner takes actions. All position requirements compare team totals. Handovers occur between rounds without a roll or penalty. Each team has one Burn for the Line, usable by the final runner. If a runner withdraws, their team withdraws.

Open Races

An Open race uses the same Ratings and course rules, but every entrant starts at 0 Progress. Announce this before entries are final. Otherwise, use Starting Marks.

Winning and Withdrawing

After the final round, the active racer or team with the highest Progress wins. Equal totals share a placing. A voluntary withdrawal takes effect immediately; the entrant takes no further actions and does not place. Recalculate neither Starting Marks nor past results. If all entrants withdraw, the race has no winner.

FIELD NOTES / 14

Race Example

A crowd gathers around the canal rail as Mika and Ren reach their starting marks. Ren’s mark is farther back. Mika spends the wait checking the ribbons across the water and pretending not to notice how quickly Ren closes the gap when the bell sounds.

Starting Marks

Mika has Pace 8. Her three highest skill Race Ratings are +2, +2 and +1. Their average rounds down to 1, giving Seed 9. Ren has Pace 11 and three Ratings of +5, giving Seed 16.

For a six-round race, Mika begins with (16 − 9) × 6 = 42 Progress. Ren begins with 0. Both race all six sections. The starting allowance does not remove Ren’s faster Pace or stronger rolls.

A Canal Crossing

On a later round, the section is Canal Teeth. Mika has Athletics Rating +2, Fatigue 0 and no Water Walking. She chooses Main route and Steady Run. Her die is 10: 10 + 2 + 2 = 14, enough to pass. She gains her Pace of 8 Progress.

Ren has Athletics Rating +5, Fatigue 1 and Water Walking. He chooses Shortcut and Push the Pace. His Stamina Rating is +3 and his accumulated penalty is 2. His resource die is 6: 6 + 3 + 5 − 2 = 12, passing Stamina 10. His accumulated Stamina penalty increases from 2 to 4.

Ren’s course die is 11: 11 + 5 − 1 + 2 = 17, below Shortcut TN 19. His Pace and sprint would give 11 + 4 − 7 = 8 Progress. He has a Spark and spends it on Second Chance. The new die is 16, producing 22 and a successful Shortcut. Ren gains 11 + 4 + 5 = 20 Progress instead.

Both totals update together. Ren has closed the gap by 12 Progress this round. Having spent his Spark on a reroll, he cannot also spend one on Burst.

A Different Skill

The next section is Archive Stairs. A runner may use Awareness or Research. Read the Route grants +7 there, so Mika can trade two Progress for a better chance of taking the Shortcut. Ren might use the same action to protect his approach, or spend resources to gain more ground. The previous section does not decide either choice.

The Final Two Rounds

Burn for the Line becomes available. Each racer can use it once, as their Racing Action. Its Stamina 15 cost may change Fatigue before the Course Check. A fast finish still requires reaching the checkpoint without exhausting yourself out of the race.
